# Family Support

Everyone is affected when someone in the family is diagnosed with cancer.

Page Content

**Family Support** has information, handouts, workbooks and web links for supporting children, teens and caregivers when cancer comes to the family.

![Talking to my family](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/coping-and-support-site/PublishingImages/health-info/coping-with-cancer/emotional-support/cancer-and-the-family/_DSC0360_lores.jpg?RenditionID=3)

**Please note:** Youth can go to another section called [Facts4Teens](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/_layouts/15/FIXUPREDIRECT.ASPX?WebId=21dc1809-d513-425f-bc0b-66947486704a&TermSetId=4b055df4-ab24-43a2-a7f2-2dfd42c28147&TermId=a91d9075-55cb-4308-9ed9-afcdacdb68b0 "Learn facts for teens dealing with a cancer diagnosis") to learn about the facts related to cancer and understand how a family member's cancer diagnosis can affect them.

My Adventures with Fox and Owl: A creative journal for kids with cancer in the family (2016) by Sara Prins Hankinson, BC Cancer Art Therapist



![My Adventures with Fox and Owl cover photo](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/facts4teens/PublishingImages/MyAdventureswithFoxOwl.jpg?RenditionID=4)This journal activity book was developed by an Art Therapist with the Patient and Family Counselling Program and an Oncology Nursing Practice Leader with BC Cancer. It is designed for children, ages 5 to 9, to help them learn creative ways of coping when a family member is living with a cancer diagnosis. [Download excerpts](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/coping-and-support-site/Documents/Support%20Programs/Excerpts%20from%20MY%20ADVENTURES%20WITH%20FOX%20AND%20OWL.pdf "Download excerpts from "My Adventures with Fox and Owl"")



Scribble and Paste: A creative journal if cancer comes to your family (2016) by Sara Prins Hankinson, BC Cancer Art Therapist

![Scribble and Paste cover photo](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/facts4teens/PublishingImages/ScribbleandPaste.jpg?RenditionID=4)

This journal was developed by an Art Therapist with the Patient and Family Counselling Program and an Oncology Nursing Practice Leader with BC Cancer. It is designed for older children, ages 10 and up, to help them learn creative ways of coping when a family member is living with cancer. [Download full copy](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/coping-and-support-site/Documents/Scribblepaste.pdf "Download "Scribble and Paste: A creative journal if cancer comes to your family"")



Reaching out to your children when cancer comes to your family: A guide for parents (2018 revised edition) by June Slakov

![Reaching Out to Your Children cover photo](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/coping-and-support-site/PublishingImages/health-info/coping-with-cancer/talking-to-my-family/Reaching%20out%20to%20your%20child.JPG?RenditionID=4)

This book has been updated recently and is for the parents, grandparents, aunts and uncles, teachers, or other support persons, who play a key role in helping children and teens who have a family member living with cancer. **[Download a copy](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/coping-and-support-site/Documents/Reaching-out-to-your-children.pdf "open reaching out book in new tab")**
