# Transportation & Lodging

Assistance with transportation and lodging associated with cancer treatment may be available for patients and their families.

For patients requiring additional assistance with travel and/or accommodation to and from BC Cancer sites, please connect with the [Patient and Family Counseling (PFC) team in your area](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/_layouts/15/FIXUPREDIRECT.ASPX?WebId=21dc1809-d513-425f-bc0b-66947486704a&TermSetId=4b055df4-ab24-43a2-a7f2-2dfd42c28147&TermId=3e7fac77-7f80-4c9a-8157-2708ae5752f5).

## Transportation

##  [Volunteer Cancer Drivers Society (Lower Mainland)](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/health-info/coping-with-cancer/practical-support/transportation-lodging#5921)

The [Volunteer Cancer Drivers Society](https://volunteercancerdrivers.ca/) is a not-for-profit organization providing complimentary transportation for Greater Vancouver and Fraser Valley residents with cancer.



Our team of volunteer drivers and dispatchers offers peace of mind for patients who may not have the means to drive to and from essential cancer treatments and medical appointments. The physical, emotional and financial burden is lifted knowing our friendly volunteers are only a call away.



**Learn more:** Visit [volunteercancerdrivers.ca](https://volunteercancerdrivers.ca/)



**Book a ride:** Call **604.515.5400**

##  [Canadian Cancer Society - Travel Treatment Fund](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/health-info/coping-with-cancer/practical-support/transportation-lodging#8213)

The [Canadian Cancer Society Travel Treatment Fund](https://cancer.ca/en/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/cancer-travel-and-accommodation-services-bc) provides funding to help reduce cancer-related travel expenses. People travelling locally may also be eligible, depending on household income.



**Learn more:**

  * Visit [cancer.ca/CTAAS](https://cancer.ca/en/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/cancer-travel-and-accommodation-services-bc)
  * Call **1.888.939.3333**
  * Access the [fund application form](https://cdn.cancer.ca/-/media/files/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/bc/bc-ttf-application-form-b.pdf?rev=db93702af81e4ab4ad93922f0f1d8879&hash=77946DCE46F98C3D841BA16DFD7CBBC0&_gl=1%2a1pi7v9h%2a_gcl_au%2aODEzODUzODY3LjE3MDU1OTU3MDQ.)

## [Canadian Cancer Society - Wheels of Hope](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/health-info/coping-with-cancer/practical-support/transportation-lodging#3414)

Wheels of Hope provides transportation to and from primary cancer treatments. Service is available in Vancouver Island, the Interior and Prince George.

**Learn more:** For more information or to register and ask for transportation, call 1.888.939.3333

##  [Hope Air](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/health-info/coping-with-cancer/practical-support/transportation-lodging#252)

If you live about a 4 hour drive or more from your cancer care and you want to travel by air, you can access free travel support from Hope Air. The Hope Air team will make flight arrangements on your behalf and cover all costs.



To learn more about Hope Air’s travel support:

  * visit [hopeair.ca](https://hopeair.ca/)
  * email [info@hopeair.ca](mailto:info@hopeair.ca?subject=Hope%20Air)
  * call 1-877-346-HOPE (4673).

## [Angel Flight](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/health-info/coping-with-cancer/practical-support/transportation-lodging#1428)

Angel Flight of British Columbia is a volunteer-led registered non-profit organization that provides free air transportation to ambulatory cancer patients who are required to travel to treatment centres on Vancouver Island and the lower mainland.



To learn more about Angel Flight:

  * visit: [angelflight.ca](http://www.angelflight.ca/)
  * email: [angel@angelflight.ca](mailto:angel@angelflight.ca)

##  [BC Bus Pass Program](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/health-info/coping-with-cancer/practical-support/transportation-lodging#8738)

The [BC Bus Pass Program](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/buses-taxis-limos/bus-pass "Learn more about the BC Bus Program") is available for low income seniors and people on disability from the Ministry of Social Development and Poverty Reduction.



**Learn more:**

##  [BC Health Connections](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/health-info/coping-with-cancer/practical-support/transportation-lodging#5582)

[BC Health Connections](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/accessing-health-care/tap-bc/health-connections "Learn more about Health Connections in BC") provides options for transportation to medical appointments. There are several programs that are available to your in your area.

##  [BC Ferries](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/health-info/coping-with-cancer/practical-support/transportation-lodging#9736)

[BC Ferries](https://www.bcferries.com/ "Visit the BC Ferries website") provides assistance for people with disabilities, assured loading cards and is a part of the [TAP program](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/accessing-health-care/tap-bc/travel-assistance-program-tap-bc "Learn more about the Travel Assistance Program \(TAP BC\)").

[Medical Assured Loading (MAL)](https://www.bcferries.com/book-sailings/medical-assured-loading) is a BC Ferries program offered for eligible patients who need to travel to and/or from a critical medical appointment by personal vehicle.

##  [Transportation Assistance Program (TAP)](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/health-info/coping-with-cancer/practical-support/transportation-lodging#1436)

The [TAP program](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/accessing-health-care/tap-bc/travel-assistance-program-tap-bc "Learn more about the Travel Assistance Program \(TAP BC\)") through the Ministry of Health offers travel discounts to eligible B.C. residents who must travel for non-emergency medical appointment not available in their own community and whose travel expenses are not covered by third party insurance or other government programs.



To be eligible, you must be a BC resident and enrolled in the [Medical Services Plan (MSP)](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/health-drug-coverage/msp/bc-residents "Learn more about the Medical Services Plan \(MSP\) for BC residents"); you must have a physician’s referral for medical services that are not available locally, escorts are also eligible if traveling with those incapable of independent travel. TAP forms are available from your family doctor, specialist’s office, or a cancer centre.



  * Phone: 604-683-7151 (Vancouver)
  * Toll-free:1-800-663-7100 (elsewhere in B.C.)

##  [Extended medical benefits](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/health-info/coping-with-cancer/practical-support/transportation-lodging#7455)

Individuals (and spouses if required as medical escort) may be eligible for assistance with treatment travel costs through their private extended health benefit plans.

## [IMT - Interior Medical Transport](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/health-info/coping-with-cancer/practical-support/transportation-lodging#3118)

[IMT](https://www.bcimt.ca/ "opens interior medical transport page in new tab") offers non-emergency stretcher transportation with medical escort for patients in the Interior. Users have to pay a fee. Call 1-250-368-7978 or email info@bcimt.ca.

## [Give a Mile ](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/health-info/coping-with-cancer/practical-support/transportation-lodging#9553)


[Give a Mile](https://giveamile.org/ "opens give a mile webpage in new tab") is a non-profit organization that provides free flights to those wishing to visit a dying loved one or flights for those who are terminally ill and want to say their final good-byes.



Fill out the [request form](https://giveamile.org/flights-for-families/ "opens give a mile request form in new tab")



Give a Mile cannot guarantee they will fulfil your request. However, their volunteers review each request with compassion and care.

##  [Fuel Tax Rebate for Persons with Disabilities](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/health-info/coping-with-cancer/practical-support/transportation-lodging#1521)

If you have been certified by a qualified medical practitioner as experiencing a permanent impairment of mobility to such an extent that the use of public transportation would be hazardous you may quality for this [rebate](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/sales-taxes/motor-fuel-carbon-tax/fuel-tax-refund "Learn more about the Fuel Tax Refund for Persons with Disabilities on the BC Government website") if you own or lease a vehicle.

##  [Vehicle insurance discounts](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/health-info/coping-with-cancer/practical-support/transportation-lodging#1754)

People who are the registered vehicle owner (or if you lease the vehicle), have a permanent disability and are considered "handicapped" under the [Motor Fuel Tax Act](http://www.bclaws.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/96317_01 "Learn more about the Motor Fuel Tax Act"), may be eligible for a 25% discount on basic Autoplan. Applicant must have a Motor Fuel Tax claim number when applying for insurance discount. Limit of $400 per year could be claimed. Call the Consumer Taxation in Vancouver.



  * Phone: 604-660-4524
  * Toll-free: 1-877-388-4440

##  [Parking at a centre and disability permits ](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/health-info/coping-with-cancer/practical-support/transportation-lodging#2927)

### Where to park at a cancer centre:

See [Parking for Patients](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/_layouts/15/FIXUPREDIRECT.ASPX?WebId=21dc1809-d513-425f-bc0b-66947486704a&TermSetId=4b055df4-ab24-43a2-a7f2-2dfd42c28147&TermId=8147bb94-3d86-4503-bd07-4737f8445a5d "opens parking for patients page").



### Parking disability permits:

Parking signs allowing people with disabilities to use designated accessible parking spaces are available in most parking facilities. Permits are available to people with permanent or temporary disabilities that impair mobility and organizations that provide transportation to persons with mobility impairments. Refer to issuer for application and process.



  * If you need a permit for Greater Vancouver, Victoria & Prince George, apply online at [Social Planning and Research Council of BC](https://www.sparc.bc.ca/parking-permits/ "Apply for a parking permit on the Social Planning and Research Council of BC \(SPARC BC\) website") (SPARC BC) website.
  * For temporary, permanent or organization permit cost is $15. Visitor permits are $25. A refund of $10 is issued upon return of permit. For more information, call 604-718-7744.
  * If you need a permit for the BC Interior, apply in person at the City of Kelowna, Revenue Branch. Permanent (3 yrs) and Temporary (1 yr) permits are available the cost is $10 plus taxes. Permits must be returned to Revenue Branch,1435 Water Street, Kelowna. For more information, call 250-469-8757.

## Lodging

### Where can you stay when you need to travel?

Resources are available for those who need assistance with accommodations associated with cancer treatment appointments.



##### Canadian Cancer Society Lodges

Cancer lodges run by the Canadian Cancer Society provide lodging for out of town patients and their families, who need to be close to a [cancer centre](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/_layouts/15/FIXUPREDIRECT.ASPX?WebId=21dc1809-d513-425f-bc0b-66947486704a&TermSetId=4b055df4-ab24-43a2-a7f2-2dfd42c28147&TermId=e7ef96f4-cc2c-4fc7-a1fe-66de33349d2f "Find BC Cancer centres and clinics") for treatment or tests. You must be independent in your daily living activities and/or have an escort to assist you.



The lodges are free for people with cancer and meals are included. There is a daily rate for a family member or support person. The lodges have limited space and reservations are recommended.

##### Medical Travel Accommodation Listings

For more information about hotels, motels and inns close to your local [cancer centre](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/_layouts/15/FIXUPREDIRECT.ASPX?WebId=21dc1809-d513-425f-bc0b-66947486704a&TermSetId=4b055df4-ab24-43a2-a7f2-2dfd42c28147&TermId=e7ef96f4-cc2c-4fc7-a1fe-66de33349d2f "Find BC Cancer centres and clinics"), refer to B.C.'s [Medical Travel Accommodation Listing](http://csa.pss.gov.bc.ca/medicaltravel/ "Browse the Medical Travel Accommodation Listing on the BC Government website"):

    * Put in the name of the city where you want to stay and you will receive a list of available accommodation
    * When you book a room, don't forget to request a medical rate.

##### Alternate lodging options

Call your cancer centre's [Patient & Family Counselling Services](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/_layouts/15/FIXUPREDIRECT.ASPX?WebId=21dc1809-d513-425f-bc0b-66947486704a&TermSetId=4b055df4-ab24-43a2-a7f2-2dfd42c28147&TermId=3e7fac77-7f80-4c9a-8157-2708ae5752f5) \- they may have a list of alternate lodging options.
