# Adolescent & Young Adult Cancer Care & Support

Every year in Canada, over 9,200 adolescents and young adults (AYA), ages 15-39, are diagnosed with cancer. AYAs have unique needs and challenges. Below is a collection of resources to help navigate these challenges and get support.

​​

## BC Cancer resources

## [Counselling](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/health-info/adolescent-young-adult#70)

Dealing with cancer can cause intense emotions. Over one third of people with cancer experience emotional distress at some point.

Individuals, couples, caregivers, and families can talk to our counsellors in-person or over the phone, or attend counsellor-led [**support groups**](http://www.bccancer.bc.ca/our-services/services/support-programs "Find a BC Cancer support program \(i.e. patient support group\)") at our regional [**centres**](http://www.bccancer.bc.ca/our-services/centres-clinics "Find a BC Cancer centre near you"), throughout cancer treatment and beyond.

Visit our [**Patient & Family Counselling page**](http://www.bccancer.bc.ca/our-services/services/supportive-care/patient-family-counselling "opens bc cancer patient and familyl counselling page in new tab") for more information and contact details.

## [Support groups](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/health-info/adolescent-young-adult#2427)

### Young Adults Art Therapy group

This group focuses on topics specific to young adults with cancer, exploring creative journaling and art exercises together. The group meets the second Tuesday of the month on Zoom and connects members with others who are the same age.

[**Register for the Art Therapy Group**](https://redcap.link/YoungAdultsGrp22 "opens registration linke for young adults group in new tab")

### Family Art Therapy Program

Support is available for families who have a parent living with cancer. Therapy is targeted to families with kids who are about 3 – 16 years old.

Individual and family art therapy sessions are also available by appointment. These are offered in person at Vancouver centre or online over Zoom. Any family in B.C. can join.

[**Register for the Family Art Therapy Program**](https://redcap.link/y2cl87dj "opens family art therapy groups registration form in new tab")

### Other art therapy groups

We have many other art therapy support groups, for all people with cancer. Check out [**Art Therapy**](http://www.bccancer.bc.ca/our-services/services/supportive-care/art-therapy#Groups "opens art therapy support groups page in new tab")**** for more information.

### Support groups

We offer other support groups for some types of cancer. Anyone is welcome to join. Go to [**Support Programs**](http://www.bccancer.bc.ca/our-services/services/support-programs "opens support programs page in new tab") and click on the calendar at the top of the page.

## [ Hereditary cancer](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/health-info/adolescent-young-adult#4217)

Hereditary cancer happens when a gene mutation that increases cancer risk is passed down from a parent to a child. Being born with that gene mutation means that a person has a higher chance to develop specific types of cancer, and may have cancer at a younger than average age.

Hereditary cancer is not common. Less than 10% of all cancer is hereditary. That means that less than 1 of every 10 cancers is caused by a gene mutation that is passed down in a family.

For more information, visit [**Hereditary Cancer**](http://www.bccancer.bc.ca/our-services/services/hereditary-cancer "opens hereditary cancer page in new tabb").

## More resources

##### Anew Research Collaborative

Anew is a research program at Royal Roads University dedicated to reshaping young adult cancer care. Visit their ****[**resources collection**](https://anewresearch.ca/resources "opens anew resources page in new tab") developed with young adults and cancer care providers.

##### BC Children's Hospital: Transition to Adult Care

BC Children's Hospital has developed the [**ON TRAC model**](http://www.bcchildrens.ca/our-services/support-services/transition-to-adult-care "opens bc children's hospital transition to adult care webpage in new tab") to help youth, families, and care providers prepare for the changes that occur in adulthood, and the differences to expect as they transfer and participate in the adult health care system.

##### Callanish

[**Callanish**](https://www.callanish.org/ "opens callanish webpage in new tab") creates a healing space for those who are living and/or dying with cancer. [Callanish offers weeklong retreats and supportive programming including for younger adults.](https://www.callanish.org/younger-adults "opens callanish young adult page in new tab")
