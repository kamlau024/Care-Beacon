## Cancer Survival Rates

Estimates for observed survival and age-standardized relative survival, confidence intervals for estimates, and trends by cancer type.

The following reports are available on survival estimates:

  * [By sex and cancer type](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/statistics-and-reports-site/Documents/survival_bysite_sex_tables_combined__20200228.pdf)
  * [By stage at diagnosis and cancer type](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/statistics-and-reports-site/Documents/survival_bysite_stage_tables_20200228.pdf)

  * [By age group and cancer type](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/statistics-and-reports-site/Documents/survival_bysite_age_tables_20200228.pdf)

  * [Conditional survival](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/statistics-and-reports-site/Documents/conditional_survival_20200228.pdf)

For up-to-date survival trends by cancer type, visit our [BC Cancer Statistics Online Dashboard](https://bccandataanalytics.shinyapps.io/SurvivalRate/).



For technical notes, please click [here](http://www.bccancer.bc.ca/statistics-and-reports-site/Documents/Survival%20Methodology%20-%2020180605.pdf).
