# Blood & Lymphoid Cancer

There are three main types of blood and lymphoid cancers: lymphoma, leukemia and myeloma.

Page Content

See the specific pages for the different types of lymphoid cancers. These are listed on the right-hand side of this page.

The [lymphoid system](http://www.nlm.nih.gov/medlineplus/ency/article/002247.htm "opens medline plus page about the lymphoid system") includes:

  * [Lymph nodes](http://www.nlm.nih.gov/medlineplus/ency/imagepages/19672.htm "opens medline plus page with diagram of lymph nodes")
  * Lymphatic vessels
  * Lymph fluid
  * Lymph tissue in organs such as the spleen, thymus, and bone marrow

The lymph nodes are small organs in many places in your body. They are mostly in your chest, neck, armpit, groin and near the blood vessels of your intestines. Lymphatic vessels carry lymph fluid between your lymph nodes and throughout your body.

For health professional information on treating this cancer, please see our [ **Cancer Management Manual**](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/_layouts/15/FIXUPREDIRECT.ASPX?WebId=21dc1809-d513-425f-bc0b-66947486704a&TermSetId=4b055df4-ab24-43a2-a7f2-2dfd42c28147&TermId=8027f965-c38e-4f70-8ffd-12fbcdf1636b "opens cancer management manual for blood and lymphoid cancers in new tab").
