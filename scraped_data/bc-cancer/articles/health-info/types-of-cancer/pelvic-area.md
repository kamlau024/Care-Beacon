# Pelvic Area

The pelvic area is the lower part of the trunk of your body between your abdomen and thighs.

Page Content

#### Pelvic area cancers include:

  * [Cervical](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/_layouts/15/FIXUPREDIRECT.ASPX?WebId=21dc1809-d513-425f-bc0b-66947486704a&TermSetId=4b055df4-ab24-43a2-a7f2-2dfd42c28147&TermId=ccb2590a-5c6e-4126-aee0-c7b060345889 "opens cervical cancer page in new tab")

  * [Gestational Trophoblastic Neoplasia](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/_layouts/15/FIXUPREDIRECT.ASPX?WebId=21dc1809-d513-425f-bc0b-66947486704a&TermSetId=4b055df4-ab24-43a2-a7f2-2dfd42c28147&TermId=65eaacfe-57c4-45f3-90d2-aefe7129e24a "opens gestational trophoblastic neoplasia page in new tab")
  * [Ovarian](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/_layouts/15/FIXUPREDIRECT.ASPX?WebId=21dc1809-d513-425f-bc0b-66947486704a&TermSetId=4b055df4-ab24-43a2-a7f2-2dfd42c28147&TermId=e8aed883-c492-476f-b5a2-56cf6274d3da "opens ovarian cancer page in new tab")

  * [Penile](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/_layouts/15/FIXUPREDIRECT.ASPX?WebId=21dc1809-d513-425f-bc0b-66947486704a&TermSetId=4b055df4-ab24-43a2-a7f2-2dfd42c28147&TermId=0cebb451-1efc-4056-99c0-2864b8c8825c "opens penile cancer page in new tab")

  * [Prostate](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/_layouts/15/FIXUPREDIRECT.ASPX?WebId=21dc1809-d513-425f-bc0b-66947486704a&TermSetId=4b055df4-ab24-43a2-a7f2-2dfd42c28147&TermId=9288dfee-d686-4d22-913c-92304363e51a)
  * [Testicular](http://www.bccancer.bc.ca/health-info/types-of-cancer/pelvic-area/testes "opens testicular cancer page in new tab")

  * [Uterine / Endometrial](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/_layouts/15/FIXUPREDIRECT.ASPX?WebId=21dc1809-d513-425f-bc0b-66947486704a&TermSetId=4b055df4-ab24-43a2-a7f2-2dfd42c28147&TermId=efcee249-3b7c-4d3c-ab78-9888a00d7f24 "opens uterine / endometrial cancer page in new tab")

  * [Vaginal](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/_layouts/15/FIXUPREDIRECT.ASPX?WebId=21dc1809-d513-425f-bc0b-66947486704a&TermSetId=4b055df4-ab24-43a2-a7f2-2dfd42c28147&TermId=d3277cf4-d709-48d1-b163-04b380e68a97 "opens vaginal cancer page in new tab")

  * [Vulvar](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/_layouts/15/FIXUPREDIRECT.ASPX?WebId=21dc1809-d513-425f-bc0b-66947486704a&TermSetId=4b055df4-ab24-43a2-a7f2-2dfd42c28147&TermId=704219d9-e039-4add-98f9-0a3524b98172 "opens vulvar cancer page in new tab")

See the [**Urinary Cancer section**](http://www.bccancer.bc.ca/health-info/types-of-cancer/urinary "opens urinary cancer page in new tab") for information about [**bladder cancer**](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/_layouts/15/FIXUPREDIRECT.ASPX?WebId=21dc1809-d513-425f-bc0b-66947486704a&TermSetId=4b055df4-ab24-43a2-a7f2-2dfd42c28147&TermId=3ba3d4f7-ae00-4519-97ae-9e910b984671 "opens bladder cancer page in new tab") and [**kidney cancer**](http://www.bccancer.bc.ca/health-info/types-of-cancer/urinary/kidney "opens kidney cancer page in new tab").



See the [**Digestive System Cancer section**](http://www.bccancer.bc.ca/health-info/types-of-cancer/digestive-system "opesn digestive system cancer page in new tab") for information about [**anal cancer**](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/_layouts/15/FIXUPREDIRECT.ASPX?WebId=21dc1809-d513-425f-bc0b-66947486704a&TermSetId=4b055df4-ab24-43a2-a7f2-2dfd42c28147&TermId=4833d69a-facd-49ec-8305-e702a30ba4f1 "opesn anal cancer page in new tab").
