# Lung Cancer

It is important to know whether a cancer in the lung is primary or secondary.

Page Content

[**Primary lung cancer**](http://www.bccancer.bc.ca/health-info/types-of-cancer/lung/lung "opens primary lung cancer page in new tab") starts in the lungs. Secondary lung cancer start as another cancer, and then moves to the lungs.

For health professional information on treating this cancer, please see our [**Cancer Management Manual**](http://www.bccancer.bc.ca/health-professionals/clinical-resources/cancer-management-manual/lung "opens cancer management manual in new tab").
