# Leukemia

Leukemia is cancer of the blood and bone marrow. There are many types of leukemia.

Page Content

[**The Leukemia / Bone Marrow Transplant Program of BC**](http://www.leukemiabmtprogram.org/ "opens The Leukemia / Bone Marrow Transplant Program of BC page in new tab")

BC Cancer, Vancouver Coastal Health and the University of British Columbia jointly sponsor the Leukemia / Bone Marrow Transplant Program of BC.

Current information about leukemia and its treatment is found on the [**Leukemia/BMT Program**](http://www.leukemiabmtprogram.org/ "opens the Leukemia/BMT program website in new tab") website.
