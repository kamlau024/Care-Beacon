# Head & Neck Cancer

Head and neck cancers start in the mouth, nose or throat.

Page Content

Revised October 2021

See the specific pages for the different types of head and neck cancers. These are listed on the right-hand side of this page.

Head and neck cancer information in this section does not include [brain cancer](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/_layouts/15/FIXUPREDIRECT.ASPX?WebId=21dc1809-d513-425f-bc0b-66947486704a&TermSetId=4b055df4-ab24-43a2-a7f2-2dfd42c28147&TermId=93a4e056-0c9f-4ae4-b52b-32f2de72c150 "opens Brain Cancer page in new tab") or [eye (or orbital)](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/_layouts/15/FIXUPREDIRECT.ASPX?WebId=21dc1809-d513-425f-bc0b-66947486704a&TermSetId=4b055df4-ab24-43a2-a7f2-2dfd42c28147&TermId=cda69dcf-7000-4936-9e8e-49aee57647a7 "opens eye cancer page in new tab") cancer.
