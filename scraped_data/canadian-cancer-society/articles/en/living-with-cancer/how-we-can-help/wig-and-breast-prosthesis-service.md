# Wig and breast prosthesis services

![A woman trying on a wig](https://cdn.cancer.ca/-/media/images/about-us/about-us-landing-page/about-us-our-stories.jpg?rev=f636ce73262042c4afe3dadcbf8dbedb&cx=0.76&cy=0.42&cw=575&ch=425&hash=51F69E522838E34422636C8E5683FD1F)

_How we can help_


All Canadians, no matter where they live, can access free wigs and breast accessories with just a simple call or click.

Canadian Cancer Society is pleased to recognize Pacific Blue Cross Health Foundation as a supporter of our Wig Banks in British Columbia.

##  Wig FAQs

Who can access/borrow wigs from the Canadian Cancer Society’s wig program?

The Canadian Cancer Society’s (CCS) Wig program provides a wide range of donated and new wigs to cancer patients, who have undergone cancer surgery or have lost their hair as a result of cancer treatment. These services are provided in English and French and can be accessed from anywhere in Canada.

What is the process to order a wig or hair accessory?

**Step 1** Client submits a request by completing the [online form](https://cancer.ca/en/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/wig-form).
**Step 2** Order form is triaged to the servicing wig hub.
**Step 3** Hub staff will email the client and provide options from our current inventory based on details included in the client request.
**Step 4** Client selects one of the options.
**Step 5** Hub will confirm the order with the client and prepare the order package that includes educational information and materials on how to care for the wig.
**Step 6** Package is shipped to client or pick-up is arranged depending on location.

How long can I borrow a wig for?

You can borrow a wig for as long as you need.

Can I borrow/order more than one wig at a time?

We lend out 1 wig at a time per client.

Is there a cost for borrowing a wig?

No. This program provides wigs, free of cost, to Canadians impacted by cancer so they can live with confidence. However, shipping charges may apply when returning your wig.

How do I order a wig?

Clients can reach our wig services through our online [order form](https://cancer.ca/en/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/wig-form) or by calling the Cancer Information Helpline at 1-888-939-3333.

How long does it take to hear back once I have submitted the request form?

It may take up 15 business days for our hub to contact you after the form has been submitted.

How will I receive my wig?

We arrange delivery by mail or arrange a curbside pick-up, depending on location. All staff and volunteers have been trained in enhanced cleaning and hygiene to make sure wigs are delivered in their cleanest state.

How do I care for the borrowed wig?

Instructions on how to care for the wig are mailed with the package. Instructions are specific to the type of wig (synthetic vs human hair).

How can I return my borrowed wig?

You will receive return instructions with your wig care package. Please follow the instructions and once you are ready to return, ship the wig to the address noted in your package. Shipping charges may apply when returning your wig.

What type of wigs do you carry?

We carry both real hair wigs and synthetic wigs.

What is the difference between real hair and synthetic wigs?

The difference between synthetic and real (human) hair wigs is in fiber. A synthetic wig can be better at holding its style even after washing. Real hair wigs are made from human hair, so they look and feel real, but they also must be restyled after washing just like real hair.

Do you have wigs for men?

Unfortunately, currently, we do not carry any wigs for men.

Can I order other accessories?

We carry a small selection of knitted toques and scarves. If you are requesting one of these items, please select “an alternate head covering” in the Wig Preferences portion of the [request form](https://cancer.ca/en/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/wig-form).

Is the wig adjustable or do I need to provide head measurements?

Most of our wigs are adjustable and we do not require your head measurements to find you the right fit.

What is the process to order a breast accessory?

**Step 4** Hub will prepare the order package that includes educational information and materials on how to care for the breast accessory.

Who can access the breast accessory program at the Canadian Cancer Society?

This service is available for free across Canada to people living with cancer who cannot afford the cost of a breast accessory.

How do I know what my breast accessory size is?

We will need your band and cup size to find you the right breast accessory.

What products do you provide?

At this time, we are only able to provide breast puffs.

What is a Breast Puff?

Also, know as a post-surgery breast form, puffs are lightweight, comfortable forms that can fill out the breast cup following breast surgery. They are intended to restore symmetry and provide shape.

Puffs are typically a fabric form, filled with a fibre-fill and made to be adjustable.

Do I need to return my breast accessory?

No, breast accessories do not need to be returned.

Is there a cost for ordering a breast accessory?

No. This program provides breast accessories, free of cost, to Canadians impacted by cancer, so they can live with confidence.

How do I order a breast accessory?

Clients can reach our breast accessory service through our [online form](https://cancer.ca/en/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/breast-form) or by calling the Cancer Information Helpline at [1-888-939-3333](tel:1-888-939-3333)

It may take up 15 business days for our hub to contact you after the form has been submitted.

How will I receive my breast accessory?

We arrange delivery by mail or arrange a curbside pick-up, depending on location. All staff and volunteers have been trained in enhanced cleaning and hygiene to make sure all your breast accessory is delivered in their cleanest state.

## Donation Information

Can I donate my hair?

We do not accept donated hair, but we will be happy to provide recommendations on where you can donate your hair.

Do you accept wig donations?

Yes, we do accept gently used wig donations. You can call us at [1-888-939-3333](tel:1-888-939-3333) to find out where you can mail or drop off a donation in your area.

Do you accept breast accessory donations?

We accept new breast puffs with tags and gently used or new bras and breast prostheses.

Are there other ways to support this program?

When you support the Canadian Cancer Society, you make a real difference to people who have been affected by cancer. Visit our [donation page](https://donate.cancer.ca/-/XZCHVVHL "donation page") to learn more.
