# Get help to quit smoking

![Two men jogging](https://cdn.cancer.ca/-/media/images/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/quit-smoking/how-we-can-help-quit-smoking-hero.jpg?rev=a042269d49b549c5a28145e4c24af11b&cx=0.5&cy=0.5&cw=575&ch=425&hash=2E2EAFAE4DD813B931AE55F1BED72586)

_HOW WE CAN HELP_


When you’re ready, we’re here to help you quit smoking or vaping.

## Get help to quit smoking or vaping

Ready to quit? Or thinking about it? Tried to quit a few times and worried you won’t stay tobacco-free for good? We are here to help.

The Canadian Cancer Society offers information, tools and a support community to help you quit and stay quit. Check out the programs available in your area.

## The Expand Project

![Expand Project logo](https://cdn.cancer.ca/-/media/images/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/quit-smoking/expand_logo-en.png?rev=c5e6b4840b014e6d9229d9ef2cd80435&hash=6586D171FAD5068A829C66F70B6EDF29)

Are you queer or trans? Do you want to join the conversation or change your relationship with smoking or vaping? The Expand Project is here for you!

The project offers free smoking cessation services and activities combined with mental health, free 4-week nicotine replacement therapy, quit coach support and a resource locator. We host bi-annual incentivized quit contests and community events and activities are hosted across 8 community partner cities across Canada.

The Expand Project offerings are available for young adults ages 18 to 29 in the provinces of Alberta, British Columbia, Manitoba, Newfoundland and Labrador, Ontario, Prince Edward Island, and Quebec.

[Learn more](https://www.expandproject.ca/)

## Talk Tobacco

![Talk Tobacco Logo](https://cdn.cancer.ca/-/media/images/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/quit-smoking/talktobaccologo_notagline_en_resized.png?h=158.667&w=238&rev=e20c4abd1b26460bb77c1b535347ec02&hash=CF4105041BFC6A7D4572B8C14EE1D835)

Are you First Nations, Inuit or Métis? Do you want to quit smoking or vaping commercial tobacco? **Talk Tobacco** is for you!

Talk Tobacco provides culturally specific, free and confidential support and information about quitting smoking or vaping. Program services were created with input and guidance from First Nations, Inuit and Métis partners, community members and healthcare providers.

Talk Tobacco services are available in British Columbia, Manitoba, Ontario and Saskatchewan. We have bilingual and interpreter services in over 16 Indigenous languages. Open 7 days a week (except statutory holidays). Hours vary by province.

**Call 1-833-998-8255 (TALK)
****Text CHANGE to 123456
******Join and follow us on****[Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/TalkTobacco)****

For services in other provinces, visit <https://csl.cancer.ca/talktobacco/en/>. Funding varies by province or territory, so services may also vary.

[Refer your clients](https://smokershelpline.ca/talktobacco/healthcare-partners/refer-a-client-or-patient) to help them quit or reduce using tobacco, smoking or vaping.

## Aspire à mieux

![Aspire à mieux logo](https://cdn.cancer.ca/-/media/images/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/quit-smoking/aspire-a-mieux.png?rev=c9cc58af9a7f4586ae47c1d0bfd9ae33&hash=58379E67BF1245DC60938AFB732C45A9)

Join the movement: stop _vaping_.

Aspire à mieux is a program designed to help young Quebec adults aged 18 to 24 quit vaping! (Available in French only.) Based on motivation and autonomy, this program gives you personalized support using several tools:

  * **Text messaging service:**
    * Prepare to quit with our 2-week prep (optional)
    * Be accompanied for 6 weeks and choose between 2 types of support:
      * Moderate (4 days of messages/week);
      * Regular (6 days of messages/week).
  * **Progressive Web App (PWA):** Access to an interactive logbook and practical tools to support you!
  * **Informative website:** Test your knowledge with our quiz and discover scientific information on vaping.
  * **24/7 Support:** Receive instant messages to motivate you and to help you manage your stress, vaping cravings, and withdrawal symptoms.
  * **Peer Support Group on Facebook:** Join our online community.

**Why stop vaping?**

  * Improve your health
  * Save money
  * Find freedom

## Build Smoke Free

![](https://cdn.cancer.ca/-/media/images/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/quit-smoking/buildsmokefree-en.png?h=150&w=400&rev=4cbea812aa994a028f6b142cf2927b3c&hash=9598516333A3D9B5C834C75A846C2E51)

Build Smoke-Free gives construction workers at certain EllisDon sites free resources and information to help them:

  * increase their attempts to quit smoking or vaping
  * get support and self-help resources
  * build social and physical work environments that support quitting smoking or vaping

Build Smoke-Free is partnership program of the Canadian Cancer Society, Ottawa Public Health and EllisDon. It receives financial support from the Public Health Agency of Canada.

> This program that you and your team have put together have given me the tools not to fail. The information and the coaches are right there for anyone to use. Better health, more money in pocket and being part of a smoke free society.

—Toronto worker on a EllisDon site

[Join Build Smoke-Free](http://www.buildsmokefree.ca/)

## Smoke Free Curious

![Smoke Free Curious logo](https://cdn.cancer.ca/-/media/images/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/quit-smoking/smoke-free-curious-en.jpg?h=147&w=238&rev=3af76447a91e42509b5571e9e8f73510&hash=15126CCDBC50EBC3268808EBA09E6A5B)

“Smoke Free Curious”, is a national and bilingual tobacco cessation social marketing campaign that aims to support Canadian adult tobacco users aged 35-64 to quit their commercial tobacco use.

**How are people successfully quitting smoking?**

Are you wondering how to increase your motivation, self-confidence and willpower to quit smoking? Are you wondering what the health, financial, and other benefits of quitting smoking are? Are you wondering how to change daily routines to live a smoke-free life? These are great questions to ask; it means you are taking action!

**You’re not alone. We can help.**

Quitting is easier with the right tools! People who use tools or resources are generally more successful in their quit attempts. Our website and social media channels offer tips, resources and motivational support from other people who are also on their quit smoking journey.

[Explore Tools & Tips](https://www.smokefreecurious.ca/s/quit-tools?language=en_US)

**Contests**

Contests are offered as incentives to help people take action towards their smoke-free goals.

[Register Now](https://www.smokefreecurious.ca/s/quit-contest?language=en_US)

**Nicotine Replacement Therapy**

When you are trying to quit smoking, Nicotine Replacement Therapy (NRT), like the patch and gum, can help to reduce the discomfort associated with nicotine cravings and withdrawal.

[Order Free NRT](https://www.smokefreecurious.ca/s/nrt)

Want to talk to someone now? Call [1-866-366-3667](tel:1-866-366-3667) toll-free to talk to a quit coach.
