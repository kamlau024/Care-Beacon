## We know how difficult it is to face a cancer diagnosis. It’s even harder if you need to travel to receive care.

Our Cancer Travel and Accommodation Services program aims to help reduce this burden through financial grants, air and ground transportation, and accommodations. Scroll down to learn more or call us at **1‑888‑939‑3333**. We’re here to help.

## Funding

Our Travel Treatment Fund provides funding to help people who need to travel for cancer treatment. People who received cancer treatment in BC, and who meet eligibility criteria based on income and distance travelled, can access the fund. Even if you’re travelling a short distance, financial support may still be available to help ease your travel expenses. Funding is also available for people who need to travel to receive a bone marrow transplant or CAR-T therapy.

To apply, select the form below based on your travel distance or preferred way to apply.

## Getting started: overview of programs in British Columbia

What are the main changes to the program and which of my patients are eligible?

With the generous support of the Government of British Columbia (BC), and with our partners at Hope Air, we’re expanding our Cancer Travel and Accommodation Services program and more of your patients will qualify for support. Residents of BC who were receiving cancer treatment in BC are eligible for support under the expanded program.

Through the expanded Cancer Travel and Accommodation Services program, we are:

  * Increasing access and eligibility for the Travel Treatment Fund while providing more funding to each eligible applicant and establishing a leukemia/bone marrow transplant grant for patients needing to travel for transplants. We have also created a grant for patients who need to travel to receive CAR-T therapy.
  * Working with our partners at Hope Air to provide air transportation, ensuring people can get the support they need to get to and from their medical appointments however and whenever they need.
  * Eliminating patients’ out-of-pocket costs to stay at our BC-based Lodges for those who are not supported by any other government program (a nominal fee still remains for caregivers). As of October 16, 2024, we will also arrange hotel accommodation if our Lodges are fully booked and a patient cannot access a room in our Lodge.
  * Expanding the Wheels of Hope volunteer driving service to underserved areas in Vancouver Island and the Kootenay region to offer more options for ground transportation.

Our goal through this expanded program is to provide as much support as possible to as many people as possible. If you have questions about how this program can best support your patients, please call us at 1-888-939-3333 or email [BCTravelFund@cancer.ca](mailto:BCTravelFund@cancer.ca).

How do the Canadian Cancer Society (CCS) and Hope Air work together to help with travel and treatment costs?

The Canadian Cancer Society and Hope Air have a long history of supporting people who need to travel to receive medical care and treatment. Our organizations offer distinct but complementary programs and services nationwide, including transportation assistance, comfortable accommodations and financial support to reduce travel costs.

We know how difficult it is to face a cancer diagnosis and that it’s even harder if you need to travel to receive care. If you live more than four hours from your cancer treatment, you may qualify for air transportation support through Hope Air. Otherwise you may qualify for one or more of the support programs offered by the Canadian Cancer Society.

## Travel Treatment Fund

Who is eligible for the Travel Treatment Fund?

Under the expansion of our Cancer Travel and Accommodation Services program, more of your patients will qualify for the Travel Treatment Fund. Some key points to keep in mind around eligibility include:

  * If your patient has a very low income, they most likely qualify for support even if they live very close to care.
  * For patients who live further from their cancer care, patients with a household income as high as $150,000 per year may qualify depending on the distance travelled.
  * The new program places less of an administrative burden on patients, as no proof of income is needed. We do reserve the right to request income verification if needed.
  * To access the Travel Treatment Fund, a patient must have received a cancer diagnosis.

How do I apply for the Travel Treatment Fund?

Please fill out an [application form](https://cdn.cancer.ca/-/media/files/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/bc/bc_ttf_application_form_en.pdf?rev=46e73d4baa3b4b5f8ce8eb4f785110dd&hash=1974D21DFD5FACF7942E7170F3E96648) and submit it by email or mail. Please review the instructions and eligibility criteria included within the form, which are based on household income and distance travelled. Depending on the distance you are travelling, the form may need to be signed by a member of your healthcare team before you submit it.

If you have further questions, please call us at 1-888-939-3333. We’re here to help.



Can I apply if I am not a British Columbia resident?

The Travel Treatment Fund is available to British Columbia residents. If you live outside British Columbia, please contact us at 1-888-939-3333 to learn about options that may be available, including Wheels of Hope and our lodges.

We’re here to help.

How often can I apply for funds?

You can apply for funds through the Travel Treatment Fund once per year, but you can stay at a Lodge or use our Wheels of Hope program throughout your cancer treatment. You may reapply for additional funds after one year if you remain in treatment and have not entered into follow-up care. If you have further questions, please call us at 1-888-939-3333. We’re here to help.

Who can help me with the application?

A trusted family member or friend can help with your application and, depending on the distance you are travelling, it may need to be signed by a member of your healthcare team. If you have any questions about the application, please call us at 1-888-939-3333. We’re here to help.

How long does it take to process my application?

Applications take approximately two weeks to process. Cheques will be sent directly to you at the address provided on the application form. If you have not received your cheque after this time, please call us at 1-888-939-3333 and select the ‘transportation’ prompt.

How much financial assistance can I receive through the Travel Treatment Fund?

The amount of financial assistance available depends on your household income and distance travelled. The eligibility criteria are available on the [application form](https://cdn.cancer.ca/-/media/files/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/bc/bc_ttf_application_form_en.pdf?rev=46e73d4baa3b4b5f8ce8eb4f785110dd&hash=1974D21DFD5FACF7942E7170F3E96648). If you have further questions, please call us at 1-888-939-3333.

We’re here to help.

Do I need to save my receipts?

No. There is no need to save your receipts as this is a grant. Once we have received your application, it takes approximately two weeks to process and a cheque will be sent directly to you at the address provided on the application form.

Can I access other funding sources along with this?

Yes, you can also access our other Cancer Travel and Accommodation Services programs alongside the Travel Treatment Fund.

Can I drop off my application instead of mailing it?

Yes, you can drop off your application in person at the address below or send it by mail. Alternatively, you can email your completed application to [BCTravelFund@cancer.ca](mailto:BCTravelFund@cancer.ca).

## Bone marrow transplant and CAR-T therapy

Has the amount of funding available for patients who must travel to receive an autologous bone marrow transplant changed?

Yes, the amount of funding available for patients who must travel more than 45 minutes to receive an autologous transplant has changed based on feedback from healthcare providers. The previous maximum amount of funding available was $2,000 and this has now changed to $4,000 based on length of stay:

  * A patient receiving an autologous transplant will initially be eligible for $2,000 to support with the cost of their stay in Vancouver
  * If a patient who received an autologous transplant needs to be away from home longer than 30 days post transplant, they can apply for an additional $1,000 of funding every 30 days, to a maximum of $4,000

What if my treatment requires me to travel to receive a bone marrow transplant?

Financial support is available for patients who need to travel more than 45 minutes to the hospital for a bone marrow transplant and includes those who have had to leave home to temporarily relocate to Vancouver for their transplant.* Financial support is also available for patients who need to travel more than 45 minutes to receive CAR-T therapy.

There is a section on Bone Marrow Transplant and CAR-T Therapy within the Travel Treatment Fund application form.

If you have any questions, please call us at 1-888-939-3333. We’re here to help.

What if my patient needs to travel to receive CAR-T therapy?

Financial support is available for patients who need to travel more than 60 minutes to the hospital to receive CAR-T therapy.

There is a section on CAR-T therapy within the Travel Treatment Fund [application form](https://cdn.cancer.ca/-/media/files/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/bc/bc_bmt_car_t_formc_en.pdf?rev=9b249fd238da485b95d6d95748dcb1dd&hash=7BDF3A2CBF0D09EAB486EA7AA8C89777). Patients travelling less than 25km can fill out an online [application form](https://cdn.cancer.ca/-/media/files/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/bc/bc_ttf_application_form_en.pdf?rev=46e73d4baa3b4b5f8ce8eb4f785110dd&hash=1974D21DFD5FACF7942E7170F3E96648). Patients travelling 25km or more should use this application form, which requires an HCP signature, and submit it by email or mail.

## Air and ground transportation

How does Hope Air help with travel?

Hope Air provides free flights, accommodations, airport ground transportation and meal vouchers for people who need to travel more than 4 hours for medical care. When a person lives four hours or more from their cancer treatment, the Hope Air Client Care team will make all flight arrangements and leverage the Canadian Cancer Society’s travel and support program where available. To learn more, visit [hopeair.ca/travel-faq/](https://hopeair.ca/travel-faq/) or call 1-877-346-4673.

For those who live closer to treatment, there may be support available through the Canadian Cancer Society. Please call us at 1-888-939-3333 to discuss what program is best for your travel needs. We’re here to help.

Can I choose to drive instead of flying?

Yes, you may choose to drive instead of fly. Funding through the Travel Treatment Fund is available to reduce any travel costs incurred, based on eligibility criteria. If you have questions about how the program can best support you, please call us at 1-888-939-3333. We’re here to help.

What if I've never flown before?

You may choose your preferred method of travel to get to your treatment, and funding is available through the Travel Treatment Fund to help reduce any travel costs incurred, based on eligibility criteria. Please call us at 1-888-939-3333 to learn more. We’re here to help.

What if my treatment is outside BC or in the USA?

Funding through this program is available if you are a resident of British Columbia and you were receiving cancer treatment in British Columbia. If you have questions, please call us at 1-888-939-3333. We’re here to help.

What if I choose alternative treatments?

Funding through this program is available if your treatment is covered under the provincial healthcare program.

Is there help if my treatment is nearby and I don’t have to travel far?

Depending on your household income, you may still qualify for financial support even if you don’t have to travel very far. You may also be able to access our Wheels of Hope program. Please call us at 1-888-939-3333 to learn more about what program is best for you. We’re here to help.

Does Hope Air have an income limit?

Hope Air uses eligibility criteria, including household income, to assess which patients qualify for support. If you have questions about which program is right for you, please call us at 1-888-939-3333. We’re here to help.

What if I need someone to drive me to my appointments?

Our free volunteer driving program, Wheels of Hope, connects people who need transportation to cancer treatments with volunteer drivers. Our drivers are trained to provide safe and reliable rides to approved cancer treatment facilities. In British Columbia, this program is available in communities in the North, Southern Interior including the Kootenays, and on Vancouver Island. To learn more about Wheels of Hope, please call us at 1-888-939-3333. We’re here to help.

## Accommodation

Where can I stay during treatment?

The Canadian Cancer Society operates seven welcoming and affordable lodges across Canada, four of which are located in British Columbia (Victoria, Vancouver, Kelowna and Prince George). The lodges provide accommodation for people with cancer and their families who must travel for cancer treatment outside of their home communities. Thanks to additional funding from the BC Government, there is no fee for patients who are not supported by any other government program to stay at our lodges. A nominal fee remains for caregivers. To learn more about our lodges , please call us at 1-888-939-3333. We’re here to help.

What if the lodge is full?

If the lodge is full, the lodge staff will add your patient to a waitlist. In the meantime, a hotel stay may be arranged. The patient stay will be covered. Caregivers will pay a flat fee of $55 per night. If a room opens at the lodge, the patient and caregiver will be required to move to the lodge. Lodge stays include three meals and on-site support.

These changes have been made to our accommodation program based on feedback from healthcare providers to reduce the financial burden on patients who are unable to stay at our Lodges when they are fully booked. We are happy to answer any questions you or your patients may have about these changes. You can reach us at 1-888-939-3333 or BCTravelFund@cancer.ca.

Can a caregiver stay with me and what is the cost?

Yes, a caregiver can stay with you at the lodge for a nominal fee. To learn more about our lodges and fees for caregivers, please call us at 1-888-939-3333. The lodges in Kelowna, Victoria and Vancouver charge a caregiver fee of $54.60 per bed per night. Thanks to the generosity of local donors, the lodge in Prince George charges a caregiver fee of $30 per bed per night.

Can my pet stay in the lodge?

Pets are not allowed to stay at the lodge unless it is a certified service dog. The lodge staff will ask for proof of certification. If you have any questions, or to learn more about our lodges, please call us at 1-888-939-3333.
