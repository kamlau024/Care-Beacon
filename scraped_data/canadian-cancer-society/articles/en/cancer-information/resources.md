# Resources

![A woman sitting at her laptop computer](https://cdn.cancer.ca/-/media/images/get-involved/volunteer/volunteer/volunteer-celebrating-main-banner.jpg?rev=648549d352df4c94badd9de1ae9eff8d&cx=0.75&cy=0.42&cw=575&ch=425&hash=D7873A1DBEA6DF14D602E8F050ED7879)


We offer a wide range of resources, including in-depth cancer information and the services and support you need during your cancer experience. Our resources can help you feel empowered and make decisions that are right for you.

## Information

We are a trusted source of cancer information that you can easily access online, by phone and with live chat.

[Contact our Cancer Information Helpline ](https://cancer.ca/en/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/talk-to-an-information-specialist "Contact our Cancer Information Helpline")[Read about different types of cancer ](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types "Read about different types of cancer")[Watch our easy-to-understand videos ](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/resources/cancer-information-videos "Watch our easy-to-understand videos")[Read our guides and publications ](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/resources/publications "Read our guides and publications")[Watch our webinars ](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/resources/webinars "Watch our webinars")[Explore the AI in Oncology information kit ](https://rise.articulate.com/share/Fety5KKDPbdpuDdkyQM1rZHW1r3moW7q "Explore the AI in Oncology information kit")[Browse our collection of healthy recipes ](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/resources/recipes "Browse our collection of healthy recipes")

## Services

We can help you find the services you need, both in your community and where you need to travel for cancer treatment.

[Find a place to stay during treatment ](https://cancer.ca/en/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/accommodation "Find a place to stay during treatment")[Find services in your community ](https://cancer.ca/en/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/community-services-locator "Find services in your community")[Get a wig or breast prosthesis ](https://cancer.ca/en/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/wig-and-breast-prosthesis-service "Get a wig or breast prosthesis")[Get transportation to your cancer treatment ](https://cancer.ca/en/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/transportation "Get transportation to your cancer treatment")

## Support

We offer a support system for people with cancer and their family, friends and caregivers.

[Talk to an information specialist ](https://cancer.ca/en/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/talk-to-an-information-specialist "Talk to an information specialist")[Join our online cancer community ](https://cancer.ca/en/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/connect-with-our-online-community "Join our online cancer community")[Find support when your child has cancer ](https://cancer.ca/en/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/child-youth-and-young-adult-supports "Find support when your child has cancer")[Get help to quit smoking ](https://cancer.ca/en/living-with-cancer/how-we-can-help/get-help-to-quit-smoking "Get help to quit smoking")
