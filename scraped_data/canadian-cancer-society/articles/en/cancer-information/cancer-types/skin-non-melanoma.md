# Non-melanoma skin cancer

## What is skin cancer?

Skin cancer starts in the cells of the skin. The most common types of skin cancer are basal cell carcinoma, squamous cell carcinoma and melanoma.

[ Learn more on What is skin cancer?](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/skin-non-melanoma/what-is-skin-cancer)

## Risks for non-melanoma skin cancer

Risks for non-melanoma skin cancer include sun and indoor tanning and having light-coloured skin, eyes and hair.

[ Learn more on Risks for non-melanoma skin cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/skin-non-melanoma/risks)

## Finding skin cancer early

The best way to find skin cancer (non-melanoma or melanoma) early is to recognize symptoms and have a personal plan for testing if you have a higher risk.

[ Learn more on Finding skin cancer early](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/skin-non-melanoma/finding-cancer-early)

## Signs and symptoms of non-melanoma skin cancer

Common signs and symptoms of non-melanoma skin cancer include a sore that doesn't heal, a sore that bleeds and scaly red patches on the skin.

[ Learn more on Signs and symptoms of non-melanoma skin cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/skin-non-melanoma/signs-and-symptoms)

## Diagnosis of non-melanoma skin cancer

Non-melanoma skin cancer can be diagnosed or ruled out by such tests as health history and skin biopsy.

[ Learn more on Diagnosis of non-melanoma skin cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/skin-non-melanoma/diagnosis)

## Grading non-melanoma skin cancer

The grade describes how cancer cells look and act compared to normal cells. Low-grade non-melanoma skin cancer tends to grow more slowly and is less likely to spread than high-grade non-melanoma skin cancer.

[ Learn more on Grading non-melanoma skin cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/skin-non-melanoma/grading)

## Stages of non-melanoma skin cancer

Staging describes how much cancer is in the body.

[ Learn more on Stages of non-melanoma skin cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/skin-non-melanoma/staging)

## If non-melanoma skin cancer spreads

Most non-melanoma skin cancers don't spread to other parts of the body. If they do, they can spread to nearby lymph nodes, lungs or bone.

[ Learn more on If non-melanoma skin cancer spreads](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/skin-non-melanoma/if-cancer-spreads)

## Prognosis and survival for non-melanoma skin cancer

Prognosis estimates the outcome for non-melanoma skin cancer. It depends on many factors including the location, size and depth of the cancer.

[ Learn more on Prognosis and survival for non-melanoma skin cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/skin-non-melanoma/prognosis-and-survival)

## Treatments for non-melanoma skin cancer

Non-melanoma skin cancer can be treated with surgery, radiation therapy, drug therapy and photodynamic therapy.

[ Learn more on Treatments for non-melanoma skin cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/skin-non-melanoma/treatment)

## Supportive care for skin cancer

Supportive care for non-melanoma skin cancer and melanoma skin cancer helps people meet physical, practical, emotional and spiritual challenges.

[ Learn more on Supportive care for skin cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/skin-non-melanoma/supportive-care)

Non-melanoma skin cancer is the most commonly diagnosed cancer in Canada. Learn about statistics for non-melanoma skin cancer.
