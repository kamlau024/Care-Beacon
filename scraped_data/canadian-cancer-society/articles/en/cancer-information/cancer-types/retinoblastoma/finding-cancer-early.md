# Finding retinoblastoma early

_Last medical review: September 2025_

When retinoblastoma is found and treated early, the chances of successful treatment are better. Take your child for regular health checkups and tell the doctor if you are concerned about your child's health.

If your child has a higher than average risk for retinoblastoma, they may need a personal plan for testing. Children with a parent or sibling with retinoblastoma or children with a known retinoblastoma 1 (RB1) gene mutation have a higher than average risk of developing retinoblastoma. Talk to your child’s doctor about tests that can help find retinoblastoma early, including the following:

  * eye exam
  * blood test to check for the RB1 gene mutation
  * MRI

These tests are also used to diagnose retinoblastoma. Find out more about [diagnosis of retinoblastoma](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/retinoblastoma/diagnosis#ci_diagnosis_of_retinoblastoma_42_10745_00).
