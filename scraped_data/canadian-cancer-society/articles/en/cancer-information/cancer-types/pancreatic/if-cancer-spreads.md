# If pancreatic cancer spreads

_Last medical review: October 2024_

Cancer cells can spread from the pancreas to other parts of the body. This spread is called metastasis.

Understanding how a type of cancer usually grows and spreads helps your healthcare team plan your treatment and future care. If pancreatic cancer spreads, it can spread to:

  * lymph nodes near the pancreas (called regional lymph nodes)
  * the peritoneum
  * lymph nodes far away from the pancreas (called distant lymph nodes)
  * the [liver](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/metastatic/liver-metastases#ci_liver_metastases_28_3928_00)
  * the [lungs](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/metastatic/lung-metastases#ci_lung_metastases_28_3926_00)
  * the [bone](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/metastatic/bone-metastases#ci_bone_metastases_28_3676_00)
  * the [brain](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/metastatic/brain-metastases#ci_brain_metastases_28_3925_00)
