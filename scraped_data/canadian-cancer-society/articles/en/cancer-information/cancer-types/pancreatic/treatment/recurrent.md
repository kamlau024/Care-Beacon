# Treatments for recurrent pancreatic cancer

_Last medical review: October 2024_

[Recurrent](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/pancreatic/staging#ci_stages_of_pancreatic_cancer_36_7710_00) pancreatic cancer means that the cancer has come back after it has been treated.

When pancreatic cancer recurs, it has almost always metastasized or grown into tissues around the pancreas, meaning that it can't be surgically removed. Because of this, doctors treat recurrent pancreatic cancer like unresectable pancreatic cancer. Treatment options for recurrent pancreatic cancer depend on how long it has been since you were first diagnosed with pancreatic cancer, what previous treatments you had and whether you are healthy enough to have treatment.

Find out more about [treatment for unresectable pancreatic cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/pancreatic/treatment#ci_treatments_for_pancreatic_cancer_36_1298_00).
