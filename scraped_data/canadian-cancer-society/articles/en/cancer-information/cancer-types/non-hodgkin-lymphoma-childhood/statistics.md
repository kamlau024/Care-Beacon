# Childhood non-Hodgkin lymphoma statistics

_Last medical review: February 2024_

It can take several years to collect and confirm cancer data, so the number of new cancer cases and deaths from recent years may not be available for some time. The most recent data available are provided.

## Incidence and mortality

Incidence is the total number of new cases of cancer. Mortality is the number of deaths due to cancer.

The most recent incidence statistics for non-Hodgkin lymphoma in children aged 0 to 14 years are from 2019:

  * 55 Canadian children were diagnosed with non-Hodgkin lymphoma.

The most recent mortality statistics for non-Hodgkin lymphoma in children aged 0 to 14 years are from 2022:
  * 2 Canadian children died from non-Hodgkin lymphoma.

For more information about cancer statistics, go to [Canadian Cancer Statistics](https://cancer.ca/en/research/cancer-statistics).
