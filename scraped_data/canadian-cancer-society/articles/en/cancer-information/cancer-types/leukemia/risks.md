# Risks for leukemia

_Last medical review: June 2025_

Some things can affect your risk, or chance, of developing cancer. Certain behaviours, substances or conditions can increase or decrease the risk. Most cancers are the result of many risks. But sometimes cancer develops in people who don't have any risks.

Different types of leukemia may have different risks, and some may be the same. Find out more about [risks for acute leukemia](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/acute-lymphoblastic-leukemia-all/risks#ci_risk_factors_for_acute_lymphocytic_leukemia_all_64_4609_00) and [risks for chronic leukemia](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/chronic-lymphocytic-leukemia-cll/risks#ci_risks_for_chronic_leukemia_00).
