# Finding vaginal cancer early

_Last medical review: January 2024_

When vaginal cancer is found and treated early, the chances of successful treatment are better. Get regular health checkups and see your doctor if you have any [symptoms](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/vaginal/signs-and-symptoms#ci_symptoms_of_vaginal_cancer_54_3101_00) or are worried about your health.

You may be embarrassed or uncomfortable talking to your healthcare team about your vagina, but you don't need to be. They are used to answering questions and addressing concerns about all body parts.

If you have a higher than average risk, you may need to visit your doctor more often to check for vaginal cancer. Talk to your doctor about tests that can help find vaginal cancer early, including the following:

  * Pap test
  * [pelvic exam](https://cancer.ca/en/treatments/tests-and-procedures/pelvic-exam#ci_pelvic_exam_89_7783_00)
  * colposcopy
  * [HPV test](https://cancer.ca/en/treatments/tests-and-procedures/human-papillomavirus-hpv-test#ci_human_papillomavirus_hpv_test_89_11090_00)

**colposcopy:**

A procedure that uses a colposcope (a lighted magnifying instrument) to examine the vulva , vagina and cervix .

Cells or tissue may be removed for examination under a microscope.
