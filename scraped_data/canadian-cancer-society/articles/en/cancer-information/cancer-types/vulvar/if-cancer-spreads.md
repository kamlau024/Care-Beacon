# If vulvar cancer spreads

_Last medical review: April 2025_

Cancer cells can spread from the vulva to other parts of the body. This spread is called metastasis.

Understanding how a type of cancer usually grows and spreads helps your healthcare team plan your treatment and future care. If vulvar cancer spreads, it can spread to the:

  * lymph nodes in the groin (called inguinofemoral nodes) on the same side of the body as the tumour or to the lymph nodes on both sides of the groin
  * vagina
  * urethra
  * bladder
  * anus
  * rectum
  * pubic bone
  * lymph nodes in the pelvis
  * [lung](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/metastatic/lung-metastases#ci_lung_metastases_28_3926_00)
  * [bone](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/metastatic/bone-metastases#ci_bone_metastases_28_3676_00)
  * [liver](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/metastatic/liver-metastases#ci_liver_metastases_28_3928_00)
