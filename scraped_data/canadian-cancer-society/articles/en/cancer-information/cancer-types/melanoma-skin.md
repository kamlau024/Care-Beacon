# Melanoma

## What is melanoma skin cancer?

Melanoma is a cancer that most often starts in the skin.

[ Learn more on What is melanoma skin cancer?](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/melanoma-skin/what-is-melanoma)

## Risks for melanoma skin cancer

Risks for melanoma skin cancer include sun and indoor tanning, number of moles and atypical moles.

[ Learn more on Risks for melanoma skin cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/melanoma-skin/risks)

## Finding skin cancer early

The best way to find skin cancer (melanoma or non-melanoma) early is to recognize symptoms and have a personal plan for testing if you have a higher risk.

[ Learn more on Finding skin cancer early](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/melanoma-skin/finding-cancer-early)

## Signs and symptoms of melanoma skin cancer

Common signs and symptoms of melanoma skin cancer include a new mark on the skin and a mole that changes size, shape, colour or height.

[ Learn more on Signs and symptoms of melanoma skin cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/melanoma-skin/signs-and-symptoms)

## Diagnosis of melanoma skin cancer

Tests to diagnose melanoma, such as biopsy, are done when symptoms suggest a problem or a doctor suspects melanoma.

[ Learn more on Diagnosis of melanoma skin cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/melanoma-skin/diagnosis)

## Stages of melanoma skin cancer

The stage of melanoma skin cancer describes the size of the tumour, if cancer is in the lymph nodes and if it has spread (metastasized).

[ Learn more on Stages of melanoma skin cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/melanoma-skin/staging)

## If melanoma skin cancer spreads

Melanoma skin cancer can spread (metastasize) to the lymph nodes, lung and liver.

[ Learn more on If melanoma skin cancer spreads](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/melanoma-skin/if-cancer-spreads)

## Prognosis and survival for melanoma skin cancer

Prognosis estimates the outcome for melanoma skin cancer. It depends on many factors including the thickness of the tumour and how fast the cancer cells are growing.

[ Learn more on Prognosis and survival for melanoma skin cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/melanoma-skin/prognosis-and-survival)

## Treatments for melanoma skin cancer

Melanoma skin cancer can be treated with surgery, immunotherapy, targeted therapy and radiation therapy. Sometimes chemotherapy is also used.

[ Learn more on Treatments for melanoma skin cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/melanoma-skin/treatment)

## Supportive care for melanoma skin cancer

Supportive care for melanoma skin cancer helps people meet physical, practical, emotional and spiritual challenges.

[ Learn more on Supportive care for melanoma skin cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/melanoma-skin/supportive-care)

## Melanoma skin cancer statistics

Cancer statistics tell us how many people in Canada are diagnosed with and die from melanoma skin cancer in a certain time frame.

[ Learn more on Melanoma skin cancer statistics](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/melanoma-skin/statistics)
