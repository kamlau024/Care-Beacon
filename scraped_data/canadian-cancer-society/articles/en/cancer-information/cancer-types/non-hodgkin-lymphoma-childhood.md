# Childhood non-Hodgkin lymphoma

## What is childhood non-Hodgkin lymphoma?

Non-Hodgkin lymphoma starts in lymphocytes. The most common type in childhood is Burkitt lymphoma. Learn about childhood NHL in our guide.

[ Learn more on What is childhood non-Hodgkin lymphoma?](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/non-hodgkin-lymphoma-childhood/what-is-childhood-non-hodgkin-lymphoma)

## Risks for childhood non-Hodgkin lymphoma

Risks for childhood non-Hodgkin lymphoma (NHL) include a weak immune system and Epstein-Barr virus (EBV).

[ Learn more on Risks for childhood non-Hodgkin lymphoma](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/non-hodgkin-lymphoma-childhood/risks)

## Symptoms of childhood non-Hodgkin lymphoma

The most common symptom of childhood non-Hodgkin lymphoma (NHL) is swollen lymph nodes. Symptoms can vary by location. Learn about symptoms of childhood NHL.

[ Learn more on Symptoms of childhood non-Hodgkin lymphoma](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/non-hodgkin-lymphoma-childhood/signs-and-symptoms)

## Diagnosis of childhood non-Hodgkin lymphoma

Tests to diagnose childhood non-Hodgkin lymphoma (NHL), such as biopsy, are done when symptoms are present. Learn about diagnosing childhood NHL.

[ Learn more on Diagnosis of childhood non-Hodgkin lymphoma](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/non-hodgkin-lymphoma-childhood/diagnosis)

## Stages of childhood non-Hodgkin lymphoma (NHL)

## Supportive care

Supportive care is an important part of cancer care. It helps children and their families meet the physical, emotional and spiritual challenges of cancer. It helps improve the quality of life of children living with cancer. It can help children to cope with cancer, its treatment and possible side effects.

[ Learn more on Supportive care](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/non-hodgkin-lymphoma-childhood/supportive-care)
