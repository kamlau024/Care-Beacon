# Thyroid cancer

## What is thyroid cancer?

Thyroid cancer starts in thyroid gland cells. The most common types are papillary thyroid cancer and follicular thyroid cancer.

[ Learn more on What is thyroid cancer?](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/thyroid/what-is-thyroid-cancer)

## Risk factors for thyroid cancer

Thyroid cancer is usually the result of different risks which include ionizing radiation and non-cancerous conditions of the thyroid.fi

[ Learn more on Risk factors for thyroid cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/thyroid/risks)

## Symptoms of thyroid cancer

Common signs and symptoms of thyroid cancer include a lump or swelling, hoarseness, a sore throat and difficulty swallowing.

[ Learn more on Symptoms of thyroid cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/thyroid/signs-and-symptoms)

## Diagnosis of thyroid cancer

Tests such as blood tests, ultrasound and radioactive iodine tests can be used to diagnose or rule out thyroid cancer.

[ Learn more on Diagnosis of thyroid cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/thyroid/diagnosis)

## Stages of thyroid cancer

The stage of thyroid cancer describes how much cancer is in the body. Different staging systems are used for the different types of thyroid cancer.

[ Learn more on Stages of thyroid cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/thyroid/staging)

## If thyroid cancer spreads

Thyroid cancer can spread (metastasize) to other parts of the neck, lymph nodes and other parts of the body.

[ Learn more on If thyroid cancer spreads](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/thyroid/if-cancer-spreads)

## Prognosis and survival for thyroid cancer

Prognosis estimates the outcome for thyroid cancer. It depends on many factors including your age and the type and stage of the cancer.

[ Learn more on Prognosis and survival for thyroid cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/thyroid/prognosis-and-survival)

## Treatments for thyroid cancer

Thyroid cancer can be treated with surgery, hormone therapy, active surveillance, radiation therapy, targeted therapy and chemotherapy.

[ Learn more on Treatments for thyroid cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/thyroid/treatment)

## Supportive care for thyroid cancer

Supportive care helps people meet the physical, emotional and spiritual challenges of thyroid cancer.

[ Learn more on Supportive care for thyroid cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/thyroid/supportive-care)

Cancer statistics tell us how many people in Canada are diagnosed with and die from thyroid cancer in a certain time frame.
