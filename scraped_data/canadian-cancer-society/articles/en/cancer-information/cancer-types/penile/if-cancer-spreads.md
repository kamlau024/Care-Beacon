# If penile cancer spreads

Cancer cells can spread from the penis to other parts of the body. This spread is called metastasis.

Understanding how a type of cancer usually grows and spreads helps your healthcare team plan your treatment and future care. If penile cancer spreads, it can spread to the following:

  * lymph nodes in the groin and pelvis
  * [lung](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/metastatic/lung-metastases#ci_lung_metastases_28_3926_00)
  * [bone](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/metastatic/bone-metastases#ci_bone_metastases_28_3676_00)
  * soft tissues
  * [liver](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/metastatic/liver-metastases#ci_liver_metastases_28_3928_00)
  * [brain](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/metastatic/brain-metastases#ci_brain_metastases_28_3925_00)
