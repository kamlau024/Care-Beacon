# If bone cancer spreads

_Last medical review: November 2024_

Cancer cells can spread from the bone to other parts of the body. This spread is called metastasis.

Understanding how a type of cancer usually grows and spreads helps your healthcare team plan your treatment and future care. If bone cancer spreads, it can spread to:

  * the lungs – the most common place where bone cancer spreads
  * other bones
  * the brain

Rarely, bone cancer can spread to lymph nodes near the tumour (called regional lymph nodes).
