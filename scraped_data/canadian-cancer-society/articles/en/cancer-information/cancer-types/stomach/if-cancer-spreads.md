# If stomach cancer spreads

_Last medical review: November 2019_

Cancer cells can spread from the stomach to other parts of the body. This spread is called metastasis.

Understanding how a type of cancer usually grows and spreads helps your healthcare team plan your treatment and future care. If stomach cancer spreads, it can spread to the:

  * lymph nodes around the stomach or in other places in the abdomen
  * spleen
  * pancreas
  * small intestine
  * colon
  * [liver](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/metastatic/liver-metastases#ci_liver_metastases_28_3928_00)
  * [lung](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/metastatic/lung-metastases#ci_lung_metastases_28_3926_00)
  * esophagus
  * kidney
  * adrenal gland
  * diaphragm
  * wall of the abdomen
  * back of the abdomen (retroperitoneum)
  * peritoneal cavity
  * omentum
  * [brain](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/metastatic/brain-metastases#ci_brain_metastases_28_3925_00)

**peritoneal cavity:**

The space between the parietal peritoneum (the membrane that lines the walls of the abdomen and pelvis) and the visceral peritoneum (the membrane that covers and supports most of the abdominal organs).



**omentum:**

A fold in the peritoneum (the membrane that lines the walls of the abdomen and pelvis) that covers and supports organs and blood vessels in the abdomen.

The greater omentum hangs down from the bottom of the stomach, covers the small intestine and attaches to the transverse colon (the middle section of the large intestine). The lesser omentum is attached to the top of the stomach and bottom of the liver.
