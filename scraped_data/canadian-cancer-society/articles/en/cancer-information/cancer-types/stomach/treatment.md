# Treatments for stomach cancer

_Last medical review: November 2019_

If you have stomach cancer, your healthcare team will create a treatment plan just for you. It will be based on your health and specific information about the cancer. When deciding which treatments to offer for stomach cancer, your healthcare team will consider:

  * stage
  * where the tumour is in the stomach
  * [HER2 status](https://cancer.ca/en/treatments/tests-and-procedures/her2-status-test#ci_her2_status_test_89_8823_00)
  * your overall health
  * what you prefer or want

Surgery and chemotherapy or chemoradiation are often used to treat stomach cancer.

## Treatments for stage 0 stomach cancer

Stage 0 stomach cancer is treated with surgery.

[ Learn more on Treatments for stage 0 stomach cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/stomach/treatment/stage-0)

## Treatments for stage 1 stomach cancer

Stage 1 stomach cancer is usually treated with surgery. Chemotherapy or chemoradiation may also be used.

[ Learn more on Treatments for stage 1 stomach cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/stomach/treatment/stage-1)

## Treatments for stage 2 and stage 3 stomach cancer

Stage 2 and stage 3 stomach cancer are usually treated with surgery followed by chemoradiation or chemotherapy.

[ Learn more on Treatments for stage 2 and stage 3 stomach cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/stomach/treatment/stage-2-and-stage-3)

## Treatments for stage 4 and recurrent stomach cancer

Chemotherapy is the main treatment for stage 4 and recurrent stomach cancer. Surgery and radiation may also be offered.

[ Learn more on Treatments for stage 4 and recurrent stomach cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/stomach/treatment/stage-4-and-recurrent)

## Surgery for stomach cancer

Surgery is a medical procedure to examine, remove or repair tissue. Stomach cancer is often treated with surgery.

[ Learn more on Surgery for stomach cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/stomach/treatment/surgery)

## Chemotherapy for stomach cancer

Chemotherapy uses anticancer drugs to destroy cancer cells. Stomach cancer is often treated with chemotherapy.

[ Learn more on Chemotherapy for stomach cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/stomach/treatment/chemotherapy)

## Radiation therapy for stomach cancer

Radiation therapy uses high-energy rays to destroy cancer cells. It can be used to treat stomach cancer.

[ Learn more on Radiation therapy for stomach cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/stomach/treatment/radiation-therapy)

## Targeted therapy for stomach cancer

Targeted therapy uses drugs to target specific molecules on cancer cells. It is sometimes used to treat stomach cancer.

[ Learn more on Targeted therapy for stomach cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/stomach/treatment/targeted-therapy)

## Follow-up after treatment for stomach cancer

Follow-up is an important part of care for stomach cancer. It often involves regular tests and visits with the healthcare team.

[ Learn more on Follow-up after treatment for stomach cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/stomach/treatment/follow-up)

* * *

**stage:**

A description of the extent of cancer in the body, including the size of the tumour, whether there are cancer cells in the lymph nodes and whether the disease has spread from its original site to other parts of the body.

Stages are based on specific criteria for each type of cancer.

The process of determining the extent of cancer in the body based on exams and tests is called staging .



**chemoradiation:**

Treatment that combines chemotherapy with radiation therapy . Chemotherapy is given during the same time period as radiation therapy. Some types of chemotherapy make radiation therapy more effective.

Also called chemoradiotherapy.
