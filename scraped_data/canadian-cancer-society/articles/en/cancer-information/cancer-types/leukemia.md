# Leukemia

## What is leukemia?

Leukemia is a cancer that starts in the stem cells of the blood. There are different types of leukemia.

[ Learn more on What is leukemia?](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/leukemia/what-is-leukemia)

## Risks for leukemia

Risks for leukemia include contact with high levels of radiation and a family history. But the risks usually depend on the type of leukemia.

[ Learn more on Risks for leukemia](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/leukemia/risks)

## Symptoms of leukemia

Symptoms may vary depending on whether you have an acute or chronic type of leukemia.

[ Learn more on Symptoms of leukemia](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/leukemia/signs-and-symptoms)

## Diagnosis of leukemia

Tests to diagnose leukemia, such as blood tests, are done when symptoms or routine tests suggest a problem.

[ Learn more on Diagnosis of leukemia](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/leukemia/diagnosis)

## Treatments for leukemia

Leukemia can be treated with chemotherapy, a stem cell transplant and targeted therapy.

[ Learn more on Treatments for leukemia](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/leukemia/treatment)

## Supportive care for leukemia

Supportive care helps people meet the physical, practical, emotional and spiritual challenges of leukemia.

[ Learn more on Supportive care for leukemia](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/leukemia/supportive-care)

## Leukemia statistics

Cancer statistics tell us how many people in Canada are diagnosed with and die from leukemia in a certain time frame.

[ Learn more on Leukemia statistics](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/leukemia/statistics)
