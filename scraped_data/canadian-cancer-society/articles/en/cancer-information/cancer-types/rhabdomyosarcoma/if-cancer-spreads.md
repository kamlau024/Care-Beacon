# If rhabdomyosarcoma spreads

_Last medical review: June 2021_

Cancer cells can spread from where they started to other parts of the body. This spread is called metastasis.

Understanding how a type of cancer usually grows and spreads helps your child's healthcare team plan treatment and future care. If rhabdomyosarcoma (RMS) spreads, it can spread to the following:

  * [lungs](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/metastatic/lung-metastases#ci_lung_metastases_28_3926_00)
  * [bones](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/metastatic/bone-metastases#ci_bone_metastases_28_3676_00)
  * bone marrow
  * [liver](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/metastatic/liver-metastases#ci_liver_metastases_28_3928_00)
  * distant muscles
  * distant lymph nodes
  * breasts
  * [brain](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/metastatic/brain-metastases#ci_brain_metastases_28_3925_00) or spinal cord (called the central nervous system, or CNS)
