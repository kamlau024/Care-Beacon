# Uterine cancer statistics

_Last medical review: November 2025_

To provide the most current cancer statistics, statistical methods are used to estimate the number of new cancer cases and deaths until actual data become available.

## Incidence and mortality

Incidence is the total number of new cases of cancer. Mortality is the number of deaths due to cancer.

Cancer statistics are currently collected, analyzed and reported based on the sex assigned at birth, with male/men and female/women categories. We need to address gaps in how statistics are collected to better understand cancer in transgender, non-binary and gender-diverse people.

An estimated 8,600 Canadian women will be diagnosed with uterine cancer in 2024. An estimated 1,600 will die from it.

For more information about cancer statistics, go to [Canadian Cancer Statistics](https://cancer.ca/en/research/cancer-statistics/canadian-cancer-statistics).
