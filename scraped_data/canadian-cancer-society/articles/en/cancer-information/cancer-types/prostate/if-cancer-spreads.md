# If prostate cancer spreads

_Last medical review: February 2021_

Cancer cells can spread from the prostate to other parts of the body. This spread is called metastasis.

Understanding how a type of cancer usually grows and spreads helps your healthcare team plan your treatment and future care. If prostate cancer spreads, it can spread to the:

  * bladder
  * urethra
  * ejaculatory ducts (the ducts that run from the seminal vesicles to the part of the urethra that passes through the prostate)
  * seminal vesicles (the glands on each side of the prostate)
  * rectum
  * pelvic wall
  * lymph nodes in the pelvis
  * lymph nodes outside the pelvis
  * [bones](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/metastatic/bone-metastases#ci_bone_metastases_28_3676_00)
  * [lungs](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/metastatic/lung-metastases#ci_lung_metastases_28_3926_00)
  * [liver](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/metastatic/liver-metastases#ci_liver_metastases_28_3928_00)
  * [brain](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/metastatic/brain-metastases#ci_brain_metastases_28_3925_00)
