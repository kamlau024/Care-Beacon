# Treatments for stage D liver cancer

_Last medical review: September 2019_

There are no treatments for [stage D](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/cancer-types/liver/staging#ci_stages_of_liver_cancer_25_1124_00) liver cancer. At this stage, the cancer is very advanced. The focus is on treatment that can ease the pain and symptoms (palliative care) of advanced liver cancer.

Talk to your healthcare team. They can help you [choose care and treatment for advanced cancer](https://cancer.ca/en/living-with-cancer/advanced-cancer/choosing-care-and-treatment-for-advanced-cancer#ci_choosing_care_and_treatment_for_advanced_cancer_102_11528_00).

## Clinical trials

Talk to your doctor about clinical trials open to people with liver cancer in Canada. Clinical trials look at new ways to prevent, find and treat cancer. Find out more about [clinical trials](https://cancer.ca/en/treatments/clinical-trials#ci_clinical_trials_89_343_00).
