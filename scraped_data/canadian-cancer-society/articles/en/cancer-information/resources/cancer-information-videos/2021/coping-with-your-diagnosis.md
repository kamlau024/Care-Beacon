# Coping with your diagnosis

![A doctor meeting with a patient in an exam room](https://cdn.cancer.ca/-/media/images/cancer-information/find-cancer-early/colorectal-cancer-screening/gettyimages-629599840.jpg?rev=9c23234b875a4d6399e2260008c3611d&cx=0.5&cy=0.5&cw=575&ch=425&hash=F1D4B27375FBF8424EBF423EF304102C)

Video


People handle the knowledge that they have a serious health problem in different ways. There is no right or wrong way to feel when you are diagnosed with advanced prostate cancer. Take the time you need to work through your emotions.
