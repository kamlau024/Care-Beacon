# Advanced cancer: A family journey

![](https://cdn.cancer.ca/-/media/images/cancer-information/resources/video-thumbnails/prostatecancer.png?rev=44d090a8b4204887ae2f4c1e82a3de1d&cx=0.5&cy=0.5&cw=575&ch=425&hash=3FF0D82E420F0D626D8DACA478799754)

Video


When a loved one is diagnosed with advanced cancer, you may feel a range of emotions. Everyone’s experience is different. Open communication can help family and friends cope with sadness and grief.
