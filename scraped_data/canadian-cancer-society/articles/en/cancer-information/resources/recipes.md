Eat well when you have cancer

Eating a variety of foods can help you get the nutrients you need before, during and after cancer treatment.

Our collection of delicious recipes can help you eat well without too much effort, even when you feel tired and unwell. Many of the recipes are simple to prepare and others freeze well so that you can have them ready for the days when you might not have enough energy to prepare meals.
