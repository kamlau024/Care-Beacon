# yttrium

## Description

A rare metal.

The radioactive form of yttrium (yttrium-90) is used in

radiation therapy

Close

radiation therapy

Treatment that uses high-energy rays or particles, such as x-rays or gamma rays, which produce charged ions or molecules to damage or destroy cancer cells.

Radiation therapy may be given from outside the body as [external beam radiation](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/resources/glossary/e/external-radiation-therapy). It may also be given as [brachytherapy](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/resources/glossary/b/brachytherapy) or [systemic radiation therapy](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/resources/glossary/s/systemic-radiation-therapy).

Also called radiotherapy, x-ray therapy and irradiation.

and some types of

biological therapy

Close

biological therapy

Treatment with natural or manufactured substances that change the way cells behave. Biological therapies may cause specific cells to stop growing, block the release of hormones or strengthen the body’s immune system.

Biological therapies are used to fight cancer or lessen the side effects associated with some cancer treatments.

Also called biotherapy or biological response modifiers (BRMs).
