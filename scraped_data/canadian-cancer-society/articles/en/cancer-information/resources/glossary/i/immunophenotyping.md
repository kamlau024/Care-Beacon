# immunophenotyping

## Description

A technique used to identify cells based on the type of antibodies that bind to the antigens on the surface of immune cells.

Immunophenotyping may be used to help diagnose certain types of leukemia or lymphoma.

* * *

**antibodies:**

A type of protein made by the immune system that disarms or destroys a specific foreign substance ( antigen ) when it appears in the body.



**antigens:**

A foreign substance that stimulates the immune system to produce antibodies against it.
