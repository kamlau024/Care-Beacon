# insulin

## Description

A hormone that lowers the level of glucose (sugar) in the blood.

Insulin is made by the pancreatic islet cells. It can also be produced in the lab.

* * *

**hormone:**

A substance that regulates specific body functions, such as metabolism , growth and reproduction.

Natural hormones are produced by glands. Artificial or synthetic hormones can be made in the lab.



**islet cells:**

A specialized cell in the pancreas that produces insulin and other hormones that help control the level of sugar (glucose) in the blood.

Also called endocrine pancreas cell or islet of Langerhans cell.
