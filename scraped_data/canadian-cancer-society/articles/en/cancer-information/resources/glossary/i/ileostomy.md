# ileostomy

## Description

A surgical procedure to create a stoma (artificial opening) from the ileum to the outside of the body through the abdominal wall.

An ileostomy creates a new path for food waste to leave the body after the colon, rectum and part of the small intestine are removed.

* * *

**ileum:**

The last part of the small intestine that receives almost completely digested food from the jejunum, absorbs more nutrients and fat, then passes digested food to the large intestine.
