# inversion

## Description

When a chromosome breaks in 2 places and a piece of DNA flips upside down and reinserts itself back into the chromosome.

* * *

**chromosome:**

The part of a cell that contains DNA ( genetic information).

In humans, each cell contains 23 pairs of chromosomes or 46 chromosomes in total.
