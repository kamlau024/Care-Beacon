# interferon

## Description

A naturally occurring substance that boosts the immune system and interferes with the ability of viruses to reproduce.

Interferons are a type of cytokine. Artificial or synthetic interferon can be used as a type of biological response modifier that can interfere with the division of cancer cells and slow tumour growth.

* * *

**cytokine:**

A substance made by cells of the immune system. Cytokines allow immune system cells to communicate with each other and thus help carry out the body’s immune response (the immune system’s reaction to the presence of foreign substances in the body).

Cytokines are produced by the body or can be made in a lab.



**biological response modifier:**

A substance, such as a cytokine , monoclonal antibody or vaccine, given to kill, control or change the behaviour of cancer cells or strengthen the body’s immune system.

A biological response modifier is a type of biological therapy .
