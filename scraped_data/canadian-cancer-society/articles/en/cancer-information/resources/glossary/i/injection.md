# injection

## Description

The use of a needle and syringe to push fluids or drugs into the body.

Injections may be given under the skin (subcutaneous), into a muscle (intramuscular), into a vein (intravenous), into an artery (intra-arterial) or into the fluid-filled space around the brain and spinal cord (intrathecal).

Commonly called a shot.
