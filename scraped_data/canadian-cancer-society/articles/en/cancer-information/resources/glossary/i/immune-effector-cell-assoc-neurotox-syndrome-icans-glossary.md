# immune effector cell–associated neurotoxity syndrome (ICANS)

## Description

A side effect that can happen after immunotherapy with chimeric antigen receptor (CAR) T-cell therapy or bispecific T-cell engagers (BiTEs).

Symptoms include headache, confusion, disorientation, problems concentrating, speech problems, changes to consciousness, seizures and muscle weakness.

* * *

**immunotherapy:**

A treatment that uses and strengthens the immune system to fight disease including cancer.

Immunotherapy is a type of biological therapy .
