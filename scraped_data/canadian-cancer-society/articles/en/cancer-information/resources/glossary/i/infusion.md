# infusion

## Description

A procedure in which a fluid, other than blood, is introduced into the body through a vein.

For example, infusion can be used to give saline (salt) solution or deliver drugs.

When infusion is used over an extended period of time, it is called _continuous infusion_.

Also called intravenous infusion.

Compare with transfusion.

* * *

**transfusion:**

A procedure in which blood or blood products are introduced into the body through a vein.

Also called blood transfusion.

Compare with infusion .
