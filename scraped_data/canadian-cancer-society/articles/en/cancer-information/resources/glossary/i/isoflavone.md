# isoflavone

## Description

A substance found in some plants (such as soybeans, peanuts, beans and lentils) that is similar to estrogen( phytoestrogen).

Isoflavones are a type of phytochemical. Researchers are studying the role of isoflavones in preventing cancer and osteoporosis (loss of bone density), as well as in relieving symptoms of menopause( such as hot flashes).

* * *

**estrogen:**

A female sex hormone that causes the female sex characteristics to develop (such as breasts) and is necessary for reproduction.

Estrogen is made mainly by the ovaries. Small amounts of estrogen are also made in the adrenal glands. It may also be produced in the lab to treat certain conditions or as a type of birth control.



**menopause:**

The time in a woman’s life when her ovaries stop producing estrogen and she has not had a menstrual period for 12 months. Most women start menopause between 45 and 55 years of age.

Menopausal means referring to or having to do with menopause, as in menopausal symptoms.

Sometimes referred to as change of life.

See also premenopause , perimenopause and post-menopause .
