# intravasation

## Description

The movement or leakage of cells or other matter (such as pus) from tissues into a blood vessel or lymph vessel.

Compare with extravasation.

* * *

**extravasation:**

The leakage or movement of blood, fluid (such as a drug) or cells from a blood vessel, lymph vessel or tube into surrounding tissues.

Compare with intravasation .
