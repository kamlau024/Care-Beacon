# intensification therapy

## Description

See consolidation therapy.

* * *

**consolidation therapy:**

Treatment given after induction therapy (the first in a series of treatments) to destroy any disease that remains in the body.

Consolidation therapy may be a more intense version of the type of treatment used during induction therapy, or it may be a different type of therapy.

Also known as intensification therapy.
