# neoplasm

## Description

See tumour.

* * *

**tumour:**

An abnormal mass of tissue formed when cells grow and divide more than they should (out of control).

Tumours can be non-cancerous (benign) or cancerous (malignant).

Also called neoplasm, mass or growth.
