# normal weight

## Description

The expected weight for a person based on the person’s height.

A body mass index (BMI) between 18.5 and 24.9 is generally considered to be a normal weight.

* * *

**body mass index (BMI):**

A measure that relates body weight to height.

BMI is used to measure underweight , overweight , obesity and normal weight .
