# non–small cell lung cancer

## Description

Any of a group of cancerous (malignant) tumours that start in the lining of the bronchi the large tubes, or airways, that carry air to and from the lungs).

Non–small cell lung cancers include adenocarcinoma, squamous cell carcinoma and large cell carcinoma.

* * *

**adenocarcinoma:**

A type of cancer that starts in the gland cells that make up the inner lining of some organs. It makes and releases mucus, digestive juices and other fluids. Adenocarcinoma is the most common type of breast, lung, esophageal, stomach, colorectal, pancreatic, prostate and uterine cancers.



**squamous cell:**

A type of epithelial cell that is thin and flat and looks like a fish scale.

Squamous cells are found in the epithelium that makes up the surface of the skin. They are in the epithelium lining of organs such as the mouth, pharynx, esophagus, anus, cervix and vagina. Squamous cells also make up the lining of blood vessels and hollow areas of the body (called cavities).



**large cell carcinoma:**

An uncommmon cancerous (malignant) type of non–small cell lung cancer in which cells are abnormally large.
