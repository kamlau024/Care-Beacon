# neurofibromatosis type 2

## Description

An inherited condition that causes non-cancerous (benign) growths called schwannomas in the cranial or other nerves of the central nervous system.

* * *

**inherited:**

Passed from parent to child through information contained in genes .

Also referred to as hereditary.
