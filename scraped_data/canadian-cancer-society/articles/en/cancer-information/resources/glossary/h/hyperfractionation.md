# hyperfractionation

## Description

Giving smaller doses of radiation therapy or chemotherapy 2 or 3 times a day (rather than once) so that a person can be given a higher overall dose.

Also called hyperfractionated radiation therapy or hyperfractionated chemotherapy.

* * *

**radiation therapy:**

Treatment that uses high-energy rays or particles, such as x-rays or gamma rays, which produce charged ions or molecules to damage or destroy cancer cells.

Radiation therapy may be given from outside the body as external beam radiation . It may also be given as brachytherapy or systemic radiation therapy .

Also called radiotherapy, x-ray therapy and irradiation.



**chemotherapy:**

Treatment that uses drugs to kill or destroy specific cells (such as cancer cells), viruses, bacteria or other micro-organisms.
