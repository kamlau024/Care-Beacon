# Hodgkin lymphoma

## Description

A type of lymphoma (cancer that starts in immune cells of the lymphatic system) that is characterized by the presence of Hodgkin and Reed-Sternberg (HRS) cells (large, abnormal cells found only in people with Hodgkin lymphoma).

Also called Hodgkin’s disease.

* * *

**lymphatic system:**

The group of tissues and organs that produce and store cells that fight infection and diseases.

The lymphatic system includes the adenoids , tonsils , spleen , thymus , lymph nodes , lymph vessels and bone marrow .

Also called the lymph system.
