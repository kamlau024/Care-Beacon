# Hodgkin and Reed-Sternberg (HRS) cell

## Description

A type of cell that is found in tissue affected by Hodgkin lymphoma. They are abnormal B cells (a type of white blood cell that makes antibodies to fight bacteria, viruses and fungi) that appear as very large cells with one or more prominent nuclei.

* * *

**Hodgkin lymphoma:**

A type of lymphoma (cancer that starts in immune cells of the lymphatic system ) that is characterized by the presence of Hodgkin and Reed-Sternberg (HRS) cells (large, abnormal cells found only in people with Hodgkin lymphoma).

Also called Hodgkin’s disease.



**nuclei:**

The part of the cell that holds the chromosomes, which contain DNA ( genetic information).

The plural of nucleus is nuclei.
