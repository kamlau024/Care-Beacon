# hemilaryngectomy

## Description

A surgical procedure to remove half of the larynx( voice box).

Hemilaryngectomy saves the person’s ability to talk because only part of the voice box, one vocal cord or part of a vocal cord is removed.

Hemilaryngectomy is a type of partial laryngectomy.

* * *

**larynx:**

The tube-shaped organ in the neck, between the pharynx (throat) and trachea (windpipe), that is part of the airway and contains the vocal cords, which produce sound.

Laryngeal means referring to or having to do with the larynx, as in laryngeal cancer.

Also called the voice box.



**laryngectomy:**

A surgical procedure to remove all or part of the larynx ( voice box).

During laryngectomy, the surgeon does a tracheostomy to place a tube in the neck to create a new path for air to reach the lungs. After surgery to remove part of the larynx (partial laryngectomy), the tube is removed and the person can talk and breathe in the usual way. If all of the larynx is removed (total laryngectomy), the person will breathe through a permanent stoma (artificial opening) in the throat and will have to learn to talk in a new way.
