# hepatoma

## Description

See hepatocellular carcinoma.

* * *

**hepatocellular carcinoma:**

A cancerous (malignant) tumour that starts in the liver. It is the most common type of liver cancer.

Hepatocellular carcinoma is a type of adenocarcinoma .

Also called malignant hepatoma.
