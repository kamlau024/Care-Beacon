# hydrocephalus

## Description

Abnormal buildup of cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) in the ventricles (fluid-filled cavities) of the brain.

* * *

**cerebrospinal fluid (CSF):**

The fluid in the cavities in and around the brain and spinal cord that helps protect and cushion these organs.
