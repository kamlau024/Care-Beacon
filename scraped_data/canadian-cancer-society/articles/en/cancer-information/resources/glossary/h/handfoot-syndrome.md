# hand-foot syndrome

## Description

A group of symptoms that include pain, tingling, numbness, redness and scaling or shedding of skin on the hands and feet.

Hand-foot syndrome is a side effect of some chemotherapy drugs.

Also called palmar-plantar erythrodysesthesia.
