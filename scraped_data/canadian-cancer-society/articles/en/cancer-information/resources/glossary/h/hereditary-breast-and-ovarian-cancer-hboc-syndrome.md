# hereditary breast and ovarian cancer (HBOC) syndrome

## Description

An inherited condition that significantly increases the risk of breast, ovarian, prostate, fallopian tube and pancreatic cancers.

* * *

**inherited:**

Passed from parent to child through information contained in genes .

Also referred to as hereditary.
