# hypertrophy

## Description

Enlargement or overgrowth of a tissue or organ caused by an increase in the size of its cells, rather than the number of cells.

Compare with hyperplasia.

* * *

**hyperplasia:**

Abnormal increase in the number of cells in a tissue or organ.

Hyperplasia can cause a tissue or organ to get bigger. It is not a cancerous condition, but it may become cancerous in some cases.

Compare with hypertrophy .
