# hairy cell leukemia

## Description

A rare type of lymphocytic leukemia in which abnormal B cells (a type of white blood cell that makes antibodies to fight bacteria, viruses and fungi) are in the bone marrow, spleen and blood. When viewed under a microscope, these B cells look like they are covered in tiny hairs.

* * *

**lymphocytic leukemia:**

A type of leukemia (cancer that starts in the blood-forming tissue in the bone marrow) in which there is an increase in the number of lymphocytes (a type of white blood cell that fights germs, foreign substances or cancer cells) in the blood and bone marrow.

Lymphocytic leukemia is chronic (slow growing).
