# herpes virus

## Description

A group of viruses that cause a variety of diseases and conditions.

There are 8 different types of herpes viruses, including herpes simplex virus( which causes cold sores and genital herpes), varicella zoster virus( which causes chickenpox and shingles), Epstein-Barr virus( which causes mononucleosis), cytomegalovirus and Kaposi sarcoma herpes virus (KSHV).

* * *

**herpes simplex virus:**

A type of herpes virus that causes blisters or painful sores on the skin.

There are 2 types of herpes simplex virus. Herpes simplex virus type 1 (HSV-1) causes cold sores and fever blisters in and around the mouth. Herpes simplex virus type 2 (HSV-2) causes genital herpes.

Also called Human herpesvirus 1 ( HHV-1) and Human herpesvirus 2 ( HHV-2).



**varicella zoster virus:**

A type of herpes virus that causes chickenpox (a highly infectious disease that causes a blister-like rash, itching, fatigue and fever) and shingles (a reactivation of the virus that causes a painful blister-like rash, usually on one side of the body).

Also called Human herpesvirus 3 ( HHV-3).



**Epstein-Barr virus:**

A type of herpes virus that causes mononucleosis (a highly infectious disease that causes fever, fatigue, malaise and sore throat).

Epstein-Barr virus is associated with an increased risk of certain cancers, including Burkitt lymphoma .

Also called Human herpesvirus 4 ( HHV-4).



**cytomegalovirus:**

A type of herpes virus that causes severe pneumonia in people with suppressed immune systems.

Also called Human herpesvirus 5 ( HHV-5).



**Kaposi sarcoma herpes virus (KSHV):**

A type of herpes virus that may cause Kaposi sarcoma and some types of non-Hodgkin lymphoma.

Also called Kaposi sarcoma-associated herpesvirus or Human herpesvirus 8 ( HHV-8).
