# hematopoiesis

## Description

The formation and development of new blood cells.

_Hematopoietic_ means referring to or having to do with hematopoiesis. For example, a hematopoietic stem cell is an unspecialized cell that can develop into any type of blood cell.

Also called hemopoiesis.
