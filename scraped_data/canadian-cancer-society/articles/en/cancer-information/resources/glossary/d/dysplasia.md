# dysplasia

## Description

Abnormal development, appearance and organization of cells so that they are different from normal cells in size, shape and organization within tissue. Dysplasia almost always refers to a precancerous condition.

_Dysplastic_ means referring to or having to do with dysplasia, as in dysplastic nevus.
