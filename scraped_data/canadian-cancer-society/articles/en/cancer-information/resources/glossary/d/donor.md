# donor

## Description

A person who gives (donates) blood, cells, tissues or an organ to be used in another person (recipient).

For example, bone marrow donors give healthy stem cells taken (harvested) from their bone marrow to people whose bone marrow has been damaged by high-dose chemotherapy or radiation therapy.
