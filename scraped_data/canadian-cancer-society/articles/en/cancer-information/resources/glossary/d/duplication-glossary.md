# duplication

## Description

When part of a gene's DNA or part of a chromosome is copied one or more times.

A duplication can be large enough that a person has extra copies of a gene or part of a chromosome.

* * *

**DNA:**

See deoxyribonucleic acid (DNA) .



**chromosome:**

The part of a cell that contains DNA ( genetic information).

In humans, each cell contains 23 pairs of chromosomes or 46 chromosomes in total.
