# diarrhea

## Description

Frequent watery stool (feces) or bowel movements.

Compare with constipation.

* * *

**constipation:**

A condition in which the stool (feces) becomes dry and hard and it is difficult to have a bowel movement. Symptoms may include painful bowel movements and feeling bloated.

Compare with diarrhea .
