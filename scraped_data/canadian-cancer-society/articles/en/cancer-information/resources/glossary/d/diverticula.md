# diverticula

## Description

Pouches or sacs that form in the lining of the intestine. They usually form in the colon, but they can also form in the small intestine.

Diverticula is the plural of diverticulum. _Diverticular_ means referring to or having to do with diverticula, as in diverticular disease.
