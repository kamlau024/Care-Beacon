# diverticulosis

## Description

A condition in which small pouches or sacs (called diverticula) form in the lining of the intestine. The pouches usually form in the colon, but they can also develop in the small intestine.

Diverticulosis doesn’t usually cause any symptoms. If the diverticula become inflamed, it is called diverticulitis.

* * *

**diverticulitis:**

A condition in which small pouches or sacs (called diverticula) in the lining of the intestine become irritated and inflamed. The pouches usually form in the colon, but they can also develop in the small intestine. Symptoms include abdominal pain, fever, nausea, diarrhea and constipation.
