# salpingo-oophorectomy

## Description

A surgical procedure to remove a fallopian tube and an ovary.

Bilateral salpingo-oophorectomy (BSO) refers to the removal of both fallopian tubes and both ovaries.

* * *

**fallopian tube:**

The tube that carries eggs from the ovary to the uterus (womb).

Also called oviduct or uterine tube.



**ovary:**

One of a pair of small, round organs in the pelvis of females (on each side of the uterus, or womb) that produce germ cells (eggs) and the female sex hormones.

Ovarian means referring to or having to do with the ovaries, as in ovarian cancer.
