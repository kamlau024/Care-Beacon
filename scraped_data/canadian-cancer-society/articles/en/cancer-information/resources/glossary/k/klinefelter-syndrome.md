# Klinefelter syndrome

## Description

Klinefelter syndrome is a genetic condition where males have an extra X chromosome. Instead of having one X chromosome and one Y chromosome (XY), they have two X chromosomes and one Y chromosome (XXY). Signs of Klinefelter syndrome include a lack of body and facial hair, enlarged breasttissue and cryptorchidism.

Klinefelter syndrome increases the risk of testicular and breast cancers.

Also called 47,XXY syndrome.

* * *

**chromosome:**

The part of a cell that contains DNA ( genetic information).

In humans, each cell contains 23 pairs of chromosomes or 46 chromosomes in total.



**cryptorchidism:**

A condition in which one or both testicles do not move (descend) from the abdomen into the scrotum. It is usually found shortly after birth.

Cryptorchidism may increase the risk of developing testicular cancer later in life.

Also called undescended testicle.
