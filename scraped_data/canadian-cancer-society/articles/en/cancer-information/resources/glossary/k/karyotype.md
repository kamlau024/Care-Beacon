# karyotype

## Description

The number and structure of the chromosomes in the nucleus of a cell.

A photograph of a person’s chromosomes.

* * *

**chromosomes:**

The part of a cell that contains DNA ( genetic information).

In humans, each cell contains 23 pairs of chromosomes or 46 chromosomes in total.
