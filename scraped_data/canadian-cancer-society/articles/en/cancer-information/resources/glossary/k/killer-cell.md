# killer cell

## Description

A type of white blood cell that recognizes and attacks foreign, infected and cancerous cells.

Killer cells develop from activated T cells (a type of white blood cell that helps control immune response, fight infection and destroy abnormal cells). They are part of the body’s immune system.

Also called killer T cell, cytotoxic T cell or cytotoxic T lymphocyte.
