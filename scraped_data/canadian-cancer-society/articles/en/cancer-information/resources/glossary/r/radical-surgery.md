# radical surgery

## Description

A surgical procedure to remove an organ affected by disease along with all nearby tissue that could contain disease.

Radical surgeries to treat cancer usually involve removing a tumour along with surrounding tissues and structures (such as lymph nodes) that may contain cancer.

Also called radical dissection.
