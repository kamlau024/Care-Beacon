# Rai staging system

## Description

A system used to describe the extent (stage) of chronic lymphocytic leukemia( CLL).

The 5 stages (I through V) in the Rai system are based on the extent of the disease in the body and the levels of lymphocytes (a type of white blood cell), red blood cells and platelets in the blood.

Compare with Binet staging system.

* * *

**lymphocytic leukemia:**

A type of leukemia (cancer that starts in the blood-forming tissue in the bone marrow) in which there is an increase in the number of lymphocytes (a type of white blood cell that fights germs, foreign substances or cancer cells) in the blood and bone marrow.

Lymphocytic leukemia is chronic (slow growing).



**Binet staging system:**

A system used to describe the extent (stage) of chronic lymphocytic leukemia .

The 3 stages (A, B and C) in the Binet system are based on the extent to which different areas of lymphatic tissue are affected and the effect of the cancer on bone marrow function.

Compare with Rai staging system .
