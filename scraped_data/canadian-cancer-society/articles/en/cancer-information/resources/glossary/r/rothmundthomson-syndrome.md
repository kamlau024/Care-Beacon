# Rothmund-Thomson syndrome

## Description

An inherited condition that causes skin rashes, thinning skin, small groups of blood vessels under the skin (telangiectasia) and bone problems. Rothmund-Thomson syndrome increases the risk of osteosarcoma, a type of bone cancer.

Also called congenital poikiloderma.

* * *

**inherited:**

Passed from parent to child through information contained in genes .

Also referred to as hereditary.
