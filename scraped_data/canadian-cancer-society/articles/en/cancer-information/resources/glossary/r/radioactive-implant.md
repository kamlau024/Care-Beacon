# radioactive implant

## Description

A small container (such as needles, catheters, wires or seeds) that holds a radioactive source and is placed in or near a tumour during brachytherapy.

* * *

**brachytherapy:**

A type of internal radiation therapy that places a sealed container of radiation substance (called an implant) to deliver radiation into a tumour or near a tumour.
