# Reed-Sternberg (RS) cell

## Description

See Hodgkin and Reed-Sternberg (HRS) cell.

* * *

**Hodgkin and Reed-Sternberg (HRS) cell:**

A type of cell that is found in tissue affected by Hodgkin lymphoma . They are abnormal B cells (a type of white blood cell that makes antibodies to fight bacteria, viruses and fungi) that appear as very large cells with one or more prominent nuclei .
