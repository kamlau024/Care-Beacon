# radiation absorbed dose (rad)

## Description

A standard unit of absorbed radiation. This measure has been replaced by the gray (Gy).

One rad equals 1 centigray (cGy) and 100 rads equals 1 gray (Gy).

* * *

**gray (Gy):**

A metric unit of absorbed radiation.

One gray (Gy) equals 1 joule (J) of energy absorbed per kilogram of matter (1 J/kg). There are 100 centigrays (cGy) in 1 gray (Gy).

Radiation therapy doses may be described by the number of grays to be delivered during each treatment.
