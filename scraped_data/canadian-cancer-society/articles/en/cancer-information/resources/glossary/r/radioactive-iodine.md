# radioactive iodine

## Description

A form of iodine that gives off radiation.

Radioactive iodine is a type of radioisotope. It is mainly used for thyroid nuclear scans, but may also be used in systemic radiation therapy.

Iodine-131 or I-131 is one type of radioactive iodine.

* * *

**radioisotope:**

A substance or element that gives off radiation.

Radioisotopes can be used in imaging tests and cancer treatments. When a radioisotope is mixed with a medicine or drug, it is called a radiopharmaceutical.

Also called radioactive isotope.



**systemic radiation therapy:**

A type of radiation therapy in which radioactive material travels through the bloodstream to reach cells all over the body.

Also called unsealed internal radiation therapy.
