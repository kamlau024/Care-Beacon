# von Hippel-Lindau (VHL) syndrome

## Description

An

inherited

Close

inherited

Passed from parent to child through information contained in [genes](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/resources/glossary/g/gene).

Also referred to as hereditary.

condition that affects blood vessels in the eye, brain, spinal cord, adrenal gland or other parts of the body, making them grow abnormally.

Von Hippel-Lindau syndrome increases the risk of kidney, adrenal gland, brain and pancreatic cancers.

Also called von Hippel-Lindau disease.
