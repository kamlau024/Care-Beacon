# Virchow node

## Description

A

lymph node

Close

lymph node

A small, bean-shaped mass of lymphatic tissue along lymph vessels (tubes through which _[lymph fluid](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/resources/glossary/l/lymph)_ travels in the body). Lymph nodes store lymphocytes (a type of white blood cell that fights germs, foreign substances or cancer cells) and filters bacteria and foreign substances (including cancer cells) from lymph fluid.

located above the left collar bone (clavicle).

A swollen, firm Virchow node may be a sign of cancer in the stomach or pancreas.
