# vasectomy

## Description

A surgical procedure to cut or block the

vas deferens

Close

vas deferens

The tube that carries sperm from the testicle to the urethra (the tube that carries semen and urine to the outside of the body).

Also called ductus deferens.

.

Vasectomy is a minor operation used to make a man sterile.
