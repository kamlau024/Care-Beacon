# vulvectomy

## Description

A surgical procedure to remove all or part of the

vulva

Close

vulva

The outer female sex organs, including the clitoris, vaginal lips (folds of skin that surround the opening of the vagina) and the opening to the vagina.

_Vulvar_ means referring to or having to do with the vulva, as in vulvar cancer.
