# oncological emergency

## Description

See cancer-related emergency.

* * *

**cancer-related emergency:**

A serious problem that occurs because of cancer or its treatment.

Some cancer-related emergencies may be the first sign of cancer. They can involve different organs, occur suddenly and be severe or potentially life-threatening.

Also called oncological emergency.
