# obese

## Description

Weighing well above the expected normal weight. Obesity increases the risk of several types of cancer, including breast, uterine, pancreatic, kidney, colorectal and liver.

A body mass index (BMI) of 30 or more is generally considered to be obese.

* * *

**normal weight:**

The expected weight for a person based on the person’s height.

A body mass index (BMI) between 18.5 and 24.9 is generally considered to be a normal weight.



**body mass index (BMI):**

A measure that relates body weight to height.

BMI is used to measure underweight , overweight , obesity and normal weight .
