# oncology

## Description

The study of cancers, including development, diagnosis, treatment and prevention.

A doctor who specializes in diagnosing and treating cancers is called an oncologist. Different types of oncologists include medical oncologist, surgical oncologist, radiation oncologist, gynecologic oncologist, urologic oncologist and pediatric oncologist.

* * *

**medical oncologist:**

A medical oncologist treats cancer using drugs and is often the main doctor for someone with cancer. They are often the ones that will make a cancer diagnosis based on the results of tests that you had. Your medical oncologist will also help you manage side effects from your chemotherapy treatments.



**surgical oncologist:**

A surgical oncologist does biopsies and surgeries to diagnose and treat cancer.



**radiation oncologist:**

A radiation oncologist treats cancer using radiation and will develop your radiation therapy treatment plan.



**gynecologic oncologist:**

A doctor who specializes in diagnosing and treating cancers of the female reproductive system, including cancers of the uterus (womb), cervix (the lower, narrow part of the uterus), ovaries, fallopian tubes (the tubes that carry eggs from the ovaries to the uterus), vagina (birth canal) and vulva.



**urologic oncologist:**

A doctor who specializes in diagnosing and treating cancers of the urinary system (including the kidneys, ureters, bladder and urethra) and male reproductive system (including the prostate, testicles and penis).



**pediatric oncologist:**

A doctor who specializes in diagnosing and treating cancers in children.
