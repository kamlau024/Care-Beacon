# B cell

## Description

A type of lymphocyte (white blood cell) that makes antibodies to fight bacteria, viruses and fungi.

Also called B lymphocyte.

* * *

**antibodies:**

A type of protein made by the immune system that disarms or destroys a specific foreign substance ( antigen ) when it appears in the body.
