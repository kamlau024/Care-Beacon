# body mass index (BMI)

## Description

A measure that relates body weight to height.

BMI is used to measure underweight, overweight, obesity and normal weight.

* * *

**underweight:**

Weighing less than the expected normal weight .

A body mass index (BMI) of less than 18.5 is generally considered to be underweight.



**overweight:**

Extra body fat that may have a harmful effect on health. Overweight increases the risk of gallbladder, kidney, uterine, colorectal, esophageal and other cancers.

A body mass index (BMI) of 25 to 29.9 is generally considered to be overweight.



**obesity:**

A condition in which a person has an abnormally high, unhealthy amount of body fat.

Obesity increases the risk of several types of cancer, including breast, uterine, pancreatic, kidney, colorectal and liver.



**normal weight:**

The expected weight for a person based on the person’s height.

A body mass index (BMI) between 18.5 and 24.9 is generally considered to be a normal weight.
