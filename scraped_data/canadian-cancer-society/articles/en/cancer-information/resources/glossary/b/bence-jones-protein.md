# Bence Jones protein

## Description

A protein that is found in the urine of people with multiple myeloma.

Levels of Bence Jones protein in the urine can be used to monitor a person’s response to treatment for multiple myeloma.

* * *

**multiple myeloma:**

A type of cancer that starts in plasma cells (white blood cells that produce antibodies to help the body fight infection) in the bone marrow.
