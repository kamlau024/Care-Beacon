# biomarker testing

## Description

The measurement of any cellular, molecular, chemical or physical change to study a normal or abnormal process in the body. Biomarker testing can be done to check the risk for, presence of or progress of a disease. It can also be done to check the effects of treatment.

Also called molecular profiling or molecular testing.
