# bacteria

## Description

Any of a variety of single-celled micro-organisms that can cause infectious diseases in humans and animals.

Examples of bacteria include streptococcus, which causes strep throat, and _Helicobacter pylori_( _H. pylori_) , which causes inflammation and ulcers in the stomach and small intestine and is associated with an increased risk of stomach cancer.

Bacteria is the plural of bacterium. _Bacterial_ means referring to or having to do with bacteria, as in bacterial infection.
