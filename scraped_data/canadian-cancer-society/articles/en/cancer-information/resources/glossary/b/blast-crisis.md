# blast crisis

## Description

The point when the accelerated phase of chronic myelogenous leukemia( CML) turns quickly into the blast phase and the disease begins to act like acute myelogenous leukemia (AML). Signs and symptoms of blast crisis include fever, tiredness, an enlarged spleen and more than 30% of cells in the blood or bone marrow are blast cells (immature blood cells).

Compare with blast phase.

**blast phase:**

The advanced phase of chronic myelogenous leukemia ( CML) in which there is an increase in the number of blast cells (immature blood cells) and abnormal white blood cells in the blood and bone marrow.

Signs and symptoms of blast crisis include fever, tiredness and an enlarged spleen.

Compare with blast crisis .
