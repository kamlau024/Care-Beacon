# biliary tract

## Description

The pathway that bile (a yellow-green fluid that helps digest fat) takes from the liver to the small intestine.

The biliary tract includes the gallbladder and the bile ducts inside and outside the liver. Bile can travel from the liver directly to the small intestine. It can also travel to the gallbladder where it is stored and then released into the small intestine.

Also called the bile tract.

* * *

**liver:**

The red-brown organ in the upper abdomen that plays a role in metabolism , removes waste products and toxins from the blood and makes bile (a yellow-green fluid that helps digest fat).

Hepatic means referring to or having to do with the liver, as in hepatic artery.



**small intestine:**

The long, tube-shaped organ in the abdomen that receives partially digested food from the stomach, absorbs nutrients and passes digested food to the large intestine.

The small intestine is the first part of the intestine. It is made up of the duodenum , jejunum and ileum .

Also called the small bowel.
