# breast-conserving surgery (BCS)

## Description

A surgical procedure to remove a tumour along with a margin of healthy tissue surrounding the tumour, while saving most of the breast.

Types of breast-conserving surgeries include lumpectomy, partial mastectomy and quadrantectomy. These procedures are basically the same – the only difference is how much breast tissue is removed.

Also called breast-sparing surgery.

Compare with mastectomy.

* * *

**lumpectomy:**

A surgical procedure to remove a breast lump or tumour along with a very small margin of healthy tissue around it.

Lumpectomy is a type of breast-conserving surgery.



**partial mastectomy:**

A surgical procedure to remove part of a breast that contains a lump or tumour.

Partial mastectomy is a type of breast-conserving surgery.

Also called segmental mastectomy.



**quadrantectomy:**

A surgical procedure to remove one quarter (or quadrant) of the breast.

Quadrantectomy is a type of breast-conserving surgery.



**mastectomy:**

A surgical procedure to remove a breast.

Different types of mastectomy include total mastectomy , modified radical mastectomy and radical mastectomy .

Compare with breast-conserving surgery (BCS) .
