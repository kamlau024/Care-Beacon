# BAP1 tumour predisposition syndrome

## Description

An inherited condition that increases the risk of cancers of the eye, skin, kidney or lining of the chest (mesothelium).

* * *

**inherited:**

Passed from parent to child through information contained in genes .

Also referred to as hereditary.
