# blinded study

## Description

A type of clinical trial or research study in which the participants, researchers or both do not know specific information (such as the study group a participant is in) that may influence them or create bias, which could lead to an error in the final results of the study.

If only the participants do not know specific information, it is called as a single-blind study. When neither the participants nor the researchers know specific information, it is called a double-blind study.

Compare with open-label study.

* * *

**clinical trial:**

A research study that tests new ways to prevent, find, treat or manage a disease (such as cancer) on eligible, informed and willing human participants.

Clinical trials are done in several steps, which are referred to as phase 0 trial , phase I trial , phase II trial , phase III trial and phase IV trial .

Clinical trials that are currently accepting new participants are referred to as open clinical trials. Those that are no longer accepting new participants are referred to as closed clinical trials.

Also called clinical study.



**bias:**

In a clinical trial , a flaw in the study design or in the methods used to collect or interpret information that can lead to incorrect conclusions about what the research shows.

For example, bias could be when a researcher or participant knows what treatment is being given, which might make them unconsciously influence the results.



**open-label study:**

A type of clinical trial or research study in which both the participants and the researchers know what treatment the participants are receiving.

Compare with blinded study .
