# biliary system

## Description

The group of organs that make, carry, store and release bile (a yellow-green fluid that helps digest fat) into the small intestine.

The biliary system includes the liver, bile ducts( inside and outside the liver) and gallbladder. It is part of the digestive system.

* * *

**small intestine:**

The long, tube-shaped organ in the abdomen that receives partially digested food from the stomach, absorbs nutrients and passes digested food to the large intestine.

The small intestine is the first part of the intestine. It is made up of the duodenum , jejunum and ileum .

Also called the small bowel.



**liver:**

The red-brown organ in the upper abdomen that plays a role in metabolism , removes waste products and toxins from the blood and makes bile (a yellow-green fluid that helps digest fat).

Hepatic means referring to or having to do with the liver, as in hepatic artery.



**bile ducts:**

The tube that carries bile (a yellow-green fluid that helps digest fat) from the liver to the duodenum (the first part of the small intestine).

Two hepatic ducts leave the liver and join to form the common hepatic duct. The cystic duct leaves the gallbladder and joins the common hepatic duct to form the common bile duct.



**gallbladder:**

The pear-shaped, muscular organ below the liver that concentrates and stores bile (a yellow-green fluid that helps digest fat).



**digestive system:**

The group of organs that work together to take in food and liquid, break them down, absorb nutrients and pass waste from the body.

The digestive system includes the organs of the gastrointestinal (GI) tract, or digestive tract. The organs of the GI tract are the mouth, pharynx ( throat), esophagus , stomach , small intestine and large intestine . The digestive system also includes other organs of digestion, which are the teeth, tongue, salivary glands , liver , pancreas and gallbladder .
