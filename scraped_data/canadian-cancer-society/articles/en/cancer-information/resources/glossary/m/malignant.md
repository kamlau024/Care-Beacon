# malignant

## Description

Cancerous.

Malignant tumours can invade and destroy surrounding tissues and spread (metastasize) to other parts of the body.

Compare with benign.

* * *

**benign:**

Not cancerous.

Benign tumours do not invade surrounding tissues or spread (metastasize) to other parts of the body.

Compare with malignant .
