# maintenance therapy

## Description

Treatment given after the first-line therapy (the first or standard treatment) to keep a disease (such as cancer) under control or to prevent it from coming back (recurring). It may be given for a long period of time.

Maintenance therapy may include drugs, vaccines, antibodies or hormones.

* * *

**antibodies:**

A type of protein made by the immune system that disarms or destroys a specific foreign substance ( antigen ) when it appears in the body.



**hormones:**

A substance that regulates specific body functions, such as metabolism , growth and reproduction.

Natural hormones are produced by glands. Artificial or synthetic hormones can be made in the lab.
