# mastectomy

## Description

A surgical procedure to remove a breast.

Different types of mastectomy include total mastectomy, modified radical mastectomy and radical mastectomy.

Compare with breast-conserving surgery (BCS).

* * *

**total mastectomy:**

A surgical procedure to remove all of the breast but not any of the nearby lymph nodes.

Also called simple mastectomy.



**modified radical mastectomy:**

A surgical procedure to remove all of the breast, most or all of the lymph nodes under the arm and the lining over the chest muscles. The surgeon may also remove part of the chest wall muscles.



**radical mastectomy:**

A surgical procedure to remove all of the breast along with chest muscles and all of the lymph nodes under the arm. The surgeon may also remove a section of rib.

Radical mastectomy used to be the most common treatment for breast cancer. Now it is only used if the cancer has spread to the chest muscles.

Also called Halsted mastectomy.



**breast-conserving surgery (BCS):**

A surgical procedure to remove a tumour along with a margin of healthy tissue surrounding the tumour, while saving most of the breast.

Types of breast-conserving surgeries include lumpectomy , partial mastectomy and quadrantectomy . These procedures are basically the same – the only difference is how much breast tissue is removed.

Also called breast-sparing surgery.

Compare with mastectomy .
