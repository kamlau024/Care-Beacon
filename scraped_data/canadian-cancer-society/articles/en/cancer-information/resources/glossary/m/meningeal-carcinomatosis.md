# meningeal carcinomatosis

## Description

See leptomeningeal metastasis.

* * *

**leptomeningeal metastasis:**

Cancer that has spread, or metastasized, from its original site to the leptomeninges.

The leptomeninges are the 2 inner layers of the meninges , which are membranes that cover and protect the brain.

Also called leptomeningeal carcinomatosis or meningeal carcinomatosis.
