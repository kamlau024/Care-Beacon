# medullary

## Description

Refers to or having to do with:

  * the middle or innermost region (medulla) of an organ
  * the bone marrow
  * the spinal cord
  * the medulla oblongata (the lowest area of the brain stem, which controls breathing, blood flow and other basic life functions)
