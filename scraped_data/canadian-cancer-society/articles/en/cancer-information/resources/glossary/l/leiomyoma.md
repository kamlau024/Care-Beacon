# leiomyoma

## Description

A non-cancerous (benign) tumour that starts in smooth muscle tissue (muscles that control organs).

Leiomyomas most often develop in the uterus (womb) and can occur in the stomach, esophagus or small intestine.

Also called myoma. Leiomyoma in the uterus is commonly called a fibroid.
