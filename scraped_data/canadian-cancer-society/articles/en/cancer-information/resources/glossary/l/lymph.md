# lymph

## Description

A clear, yellowish fluid that contains nutrients, lymphocytes (a type of white blood cell that fights germs, foreign substances or cancer cells) and antibodies. Lymph circulates throughout the body in lymph vessels and bathes body tissues.

Also called lymph fluid or lymphatic fluid.

* * *

**antibodies:**

A type of protein made by the immune system that disarms or destroys a specific foreign substance ( antigen ) when it appears in the body.
