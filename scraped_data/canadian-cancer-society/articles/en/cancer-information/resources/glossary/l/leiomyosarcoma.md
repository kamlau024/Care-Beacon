# leiomyosarcoma

## Description

A cancerous (malignant) tumour that starts in smooth muscle tissue (muscles that control organs).

Leiomyosarcomas can develop almost anywhere in the body, but they usually develop in the uterus (womb), gastrointestinal (GI) tract or pelvis.
