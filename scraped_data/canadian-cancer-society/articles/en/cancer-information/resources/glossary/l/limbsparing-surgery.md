# limb-sparing surgery

## Description

A surgical procedure to remove a tumour along with a margin of healthy tissue surrounding the tumour, while avoiding amputation and saving most of the limb.

Limb-sparing surgery usually involves reconstructing the limb using a prosthesis or bone, muscle or soft-tissue graft.
