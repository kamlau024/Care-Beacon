# luteinizing hormone

## Description

A hormone that is involved in reproduction. In females, it makes the ovaries release eggs and hormones that get the uterus (womb) ready to receive a fertilized egg. In males, it makes the testicles produce testosterone.

Luteinizing hormone is made by the pituitary gland.

Also called interstitial cell-stimulating hormone and lutropin.

* * *

**hormone:**

A substance that regulates specific body functions, such as metabolism , growth and reproduction.

Natural hormones are produced by glands. Artificial or synthetic hormones can be made in the lab.



**pituitary gland:**

The main endocrine system gland at the base of the brain that produces hormones to control other glands and many body functions, including growth.

Also called the hypophysis.
