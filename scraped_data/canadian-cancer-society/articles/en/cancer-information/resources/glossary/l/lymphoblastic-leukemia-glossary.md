# lymphoblastic leukemia

## Description

A type of leukemia (cancer that starts in the blood-forming tissue in the bone marrow) in which there is an increase in the number of lymphoblasts (immature lymphocytes) in the blood and bone marrow.

Lymphoblastic leukemia is acute (fast growing and aggressive).

* * *

**lymphocytes:**

A type of white blood cell that fights viruses, bacteria, foreign substances or abnormal cells (including cancer cells).

The 3 types of lymphocytes are B cells , T cells and natural killer (NK) cells .
