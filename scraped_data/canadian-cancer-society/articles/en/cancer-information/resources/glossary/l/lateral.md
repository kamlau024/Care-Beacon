# lateral

## Description

Referring to or having to do with a side (left or right), away from the middle of the body or a structure.

Compare with medial.

* * *

**medial:**

Referring to, having to do with, toward or nearer the middle of the body.

Referring to or having to do with the middle layer of a structure.

Compare with lateral .
