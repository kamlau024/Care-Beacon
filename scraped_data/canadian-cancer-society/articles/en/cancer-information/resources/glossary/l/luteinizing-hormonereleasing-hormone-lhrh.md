# luteinizing hormone–releasing hormone (LHRH)

## Description

A hormone that controls the production of sex hormones in males and females.

The hypothalamus produces luteinizing hormone–releasing hormone (LHRH), which stimulates the pituitary gland to produce luteinizing hormone (LH). In turn, LH stimulates the testicles to produce testosterone and the ovaries to produce estrogen and progesterone.

Also called gonadotropin-releasing hormone.

* * *

**hormone:**

A substance that regulates specific body functions, such as metabolism , growth and reproduction.

Natural hormones are produced by glands. Artificial or synthetic hormones can be made in the lab.



**hypothalamus:**

The part of the brain below the thalamus that controls hormone production, water balance, body temperature and other activities in the body related to metabolism .



**pituitary gland:**

The main endocrine system gland at the base of the brain that produces hormones to control other glands and many body functions, including growth.

Also called the hypophysis.
