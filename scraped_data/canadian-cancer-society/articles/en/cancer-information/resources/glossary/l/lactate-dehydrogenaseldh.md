# lactate dehydrogenase (LDH)

## Description

An enzyme that is involved in energy production in cells.

LDH is normally found in the blood and other body tissues. Higher levels of LDH may indicate tissue damage or the presence of cancer cells. LDH may be used as a tumour marker or to monitor a person’s response to treatment for certain cancers.

Also called lactic acid dehyrodgenase.

* * *

**enzyme:**

A protein that speeds up certain chemical reactions in the body.

For example, enzymes in the intestines help to digest food.



**tumour marker:**

A substance in the body that may indicate the presence of a certain type of cancer.

Tumour markers may be produced by cancer cells or by the body in response to the cancer. They can be found in blood or other body fluids and may be used to detect cancer or monitor a person’s response to treatment.

For example, prostate-specific antigen (PSA) can be used as a tumour marker for prostate cancer.
