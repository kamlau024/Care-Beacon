# cancer

## Description

A group of more than 100 diseases that cause abnormal cells to grow and divide out of control.

Cancer cells can grow into surrounding tissue and spread to other parts of the body through the blood or lymphatic system.

* * *

**lymphatic system:**

The group of tissues and organs that produce and store cells that fight infection and diseases.

The lymphatic system includes the adenoids , tonsils , spleen , thymus , lymph nodes , lymph vessels and bone marrow .

Also called the lymph system.
