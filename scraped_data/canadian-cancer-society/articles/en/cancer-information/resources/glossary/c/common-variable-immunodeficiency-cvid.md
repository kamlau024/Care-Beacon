# common variable immunodeficiency (CVID)

## Description

A disorder that damages the immune system. People with CVID are at a higher risk of infections, gastrointestinal inflammation and autoimmune diseases such as rheumatoid arthritis.

CVID increases the risk of non-Hodgkin lymphoma.
