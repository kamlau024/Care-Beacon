# carcinosarcoma

## Description

A rare cancerous (malignant) tumour that is a mixture of carcinoma (cancer that starts in the skin or in tissues that line or cover body organs) and sarcoma(cancer that starts in connective tissue that surrounds and supports various organs in the body).
