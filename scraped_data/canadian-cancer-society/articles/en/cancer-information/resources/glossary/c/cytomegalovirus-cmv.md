# cytomegalovirus (CMV)

## Description

A type of herpes virus that causes severe pneumonia in people with suppressed immune systems.

Also called _Human herpesvirus 5_( HHV-5).

* * *

**pneumonia:**

Infection of one or both lungs caused by bacteria, viruses or fungi. Signs and symptoms include fever, chills, cough with sputum (phlegm), chest pain and shortness of breath.
