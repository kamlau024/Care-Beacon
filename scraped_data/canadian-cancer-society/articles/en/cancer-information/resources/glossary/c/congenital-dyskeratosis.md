# congenital dyskeratosis

## Description

An inherited condition that causes abnormally shaped fingernails and toenails, a lacy rash on the face and chest, white patches in the mouth and often bone marrow failure. Congenital dyskeratosis increases the risk of myelodysplastic syndromes (MDS), head and neck cancers and acute myelogenous leukemia (AML).

* * *

**inherited:**

Passed from parent to child through information contained in genes .

Also referred to as hereditary.
