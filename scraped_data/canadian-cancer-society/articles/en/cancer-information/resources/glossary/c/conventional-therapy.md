# conventional therapy

## Description

Treatment that is currently accepted and widely used in the Canadian healthcare system for a certain disease (such as cancer).

Healthcare professionals use conventional therapies because the best available scientific research has shown them to be safe and effective.

Also called conventional treatment, conventional medicine or mainstream medicine.
