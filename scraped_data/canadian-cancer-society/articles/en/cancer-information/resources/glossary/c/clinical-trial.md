# clinical trial

## Description

A research study that tests new ways to prevent, find, treat or manage a disease (such as cancer) on eligible, informed and willing human participants.

Clinical trials are done in several steps, which are referred to as phase 0 trial, phase I trial, phase II trial, phase III trial and phase IV trial.

Clinical trials that are currently accepting new participants are referred to as open clinical trials. Those that are no longer accepting new participants are referred to as closed clinical trials.

Also called clinical study.

* * *

**phase 0 trial:**

A trial that is sometimes done before a phase I trial in which a very small dose of a drug is given to a small group of people to study how the drug is used by and affects the human body.



**phase I trial:**

The first step in a clinical trial in which researchers study the safety and side effects of a new treatment, the best way to give the treatment (for example, by mouth, injection or intravenously) and the best dose (the highest dose that does not cause harmful side effects). Phase I trials include a small group of participants.



**phase II trial:**

The second step in a clinical trial in which researchers determine if a new treatment works for a specific type of disease (such as cancer) and continues to study how safe the treatment is and its possible side effects. A phase II trial includes more participants than a phase I trial.



**phase III trial:**

The third step in a clinical trial in which researchers compare the results of people taking a new treatment with the results of people taking the standard treatment ( for example, which group has fewer side effects or better survival rates). A phase III trial includes more participants than a phase I or phase II trial.



**phase IV trial:**

The fourth step in a clinical trial in which researchers study a new treatment that has been approved and is being marketed to better understand how often there are negative side effects and the risks and benefits of long-term use. A phase IV trial includes many more participants than the earlier phases.
