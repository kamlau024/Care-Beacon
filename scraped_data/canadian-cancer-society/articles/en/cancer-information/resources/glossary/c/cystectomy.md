# cystectomy

## Description

A cystectomy is a surgical procedure to remove all or part of the bladder. It is also a surgical procedure to remove a cyst (a sac in the body that is usually filled with fluid or semi-solid material).

* * *

**bladder:**

The muscular sac in the pelvis that receives urine from the ureters (tubes that carry urine from the kidneys), stores it and passes it from the body through the urethra.
