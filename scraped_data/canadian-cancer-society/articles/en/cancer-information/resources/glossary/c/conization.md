# conization

## Description

See cone biopsy.

* * *

**cone biopsy:**

A type of biopsy in which a cone-shaped piece of tissue is surgically removed from the cervix (the lower, narrow part of the uterus, or womb) for examination under a microscope.

Cone biopsy may be used to diagnose or treat cervical conditions.

Also called conization.
