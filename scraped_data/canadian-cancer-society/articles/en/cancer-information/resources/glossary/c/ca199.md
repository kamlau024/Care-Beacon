# CA19-9

## Description

See carbohydrate antigen 19-9.

* * *

**carbohydrate antigen 19-9:**

A protein that may be found in high amounts in the blood of people with certain types of cancer, particularly advanced pancreatic cancer.

CA19-9 can be used as a tumour marker . It is used to monitor a person’s response to treatment or to see if pancreatic cancer has come back (recurred) after treatment.
