# Cushing syndrome

## Description

A group of symptoms that include high blood pressure, high blood sugar, round face, thin arms and legs, fatigue, muscle weakness and weight gain (which may cause purple stretch marks on the stomach).

Cushing syndrome occurs when the body produces too much corticosteroids( usually due to a benign tumour of the adrenal gland) or a person uses steroid drugs.

* * *

**corticosteroids:**

Any steroid hormone that acts as an anti-inflammatory by reducing swelling and lowering the body’s immune response (the immune system’s reaction to the presence of foreign substances).

Corticosteroids are made by the adrenal gland . They can also be produced in the lab.
