# complement system

## Description

A group of proteins in the body that can be activated by the immune system to help antibodies destroy antigens, attract phagocytes (a type of white blood cell) to bacteria or micro-organisms, and kill bacteria and other foreign substances.

* * *

**antibodies:**

A type of protein made by the immune system that disarms or destroys a specific foreign substance ( antigen ) when it appears in the body.



**antigens:**

A foreign substance that stimulates the immune system to produce antibodies against it.
