# CA125

## Description

See cancer antigen 125.

* * *

**cancer antigen 125:**

A protein that may be found in high amounts in the blood of people with certain types of cancer, particularly ovarian cancer.

CA125 can be used as a tumour marker . It is used to monitor a person’s response to treatment or to see if ovarian cancer has come back (recurred) after treatment.

Also called carbohydrate antigen 125.
