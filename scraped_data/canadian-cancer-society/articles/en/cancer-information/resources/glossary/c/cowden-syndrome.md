# Cowden syndrome

## Description

An inherited condition in which many non-cancerous (benign) growths (hamartomas) form in the skin, breast, thyroid, colon, intestines and inside of the mouth.

Cowden syndrome is associated with an increased risk of some cancers, including breast and thyroid cancers.

Also called Cowden disease and multiple hamartoma syndrome.

* * *

**inherited:**

Passed from parent to child through information contained in genes .

Also referred to as hereditary.
