# calcitonin

## Description

A hormone that helps to regulate the level of calcium in the blood by acting on the kidneys and slowing or stopping bone resorption (the breakdown of bone and absorption of the calcium released into the blood).

Calcitonin is made by the thyroid.

* * *

**hormone:**

A substance that regulates specific body functions, such as metabolism , growth and reproduction.

Natural hormones are produced by glands. Artificial or synthetic hormones can be made in the lab.



**thyroid:**

The gland in the neck, on either side of the trachea (windpipe) just below the larynx (voice box), that makes and releases hormones involved in growth and metabolism .

Also called the thyroid gland.
