# corticosteroid

## Description

Any steroid hormone that acts as an anti-inflammatory by reducing swelling and lowering the body’s immune response (the immune system’s reaction to the presence of foreign substances).

Corticosteroids are made by the adrenal gland. They can also be produced in the lab.

* * *

**hormone:**

A substance that regulates specific body functions, such as metabolism , growth and reproduction.

Natural hormones are produced by glands. Artificial or synthetic hormones can be made in the lab.



**adrenal gland:**

A small gland on top of each kidney that produces a variety of hormones involved in different body functions, including metabolism (the chemical processes needed for cell function, growth and reproduction), heart rate, blood pressure and controlling blood sugar levels.
