# chondrosarcoma

## Description

A cancerous (malignant) tumour that starts in cartilage cells.

Chondrosarcomas most often develop in the hips, legs, shoulders or ribs.

* * *

**cartilage:**

A type of tough, flexible connective tissue that lines the joints and gives structure to the nose, ears, larynx and other body parts.
