# CA15-3

## Description

See cancer antigen 15-3.

* * *

**cancer antigen 15-3:**

A protein that may be found in high amounts in the blood of people with certain types of cancer, particularly breast cancer.

CA15-3 can be used as a tumour marker . It is used to monitor a person’s response to treatment or to see if breast cancer has come back (recurred) after treatment.

Also called carbohydrate antigen 15-3.
