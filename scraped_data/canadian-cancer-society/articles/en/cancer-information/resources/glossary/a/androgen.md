# androgen

## Description

A type of hormone that regulates the growth, development and function of the male reproductive system (the group of organs involved in having children). Androgens also control the development of male physical traits, such as deep voice and the growth of body and facial hair, and increase muscle and bone strength.

The main androgen is testosterone, which is made mainly in the testicles.

* * *

**hormone:**

A substance that regulates specific body functions, such as metabolism , growth and reproduction.

Natural hormones are produced by glands. Artificial or synthetic hormones can be made in the lab.



**testosterone:**

A male sex hormone that causes male sex characteristics to develop (such as deep voice and facial hair) and stimulates the development of the male reproductive system and sexual activity.

Testosterone is made mainly by the testicles. Small amounts of testosterone are also made in the adrenal glands. It may also be produced in the lab to treat certain conditions.
