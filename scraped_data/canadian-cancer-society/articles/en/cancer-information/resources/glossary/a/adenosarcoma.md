# adenosarcoma

## Description

A cancerous (malignant) tumour that is a mix of adenoma and sarcoma.

An example of adenosarcoma is Wilms’ tumour.

* * *

**adenoma:**

A non-cancerous, or benign, tumour or growth that starts in epithelial cells that line the inside of organs and glands. Adenomas may become cancerous if they are not treated.

An adenoma may be called an adenomatous polyp when it is in the gastrointestinal (GI) tract.



**sarcoma:**

A type of cancer that starts in connective tissues (tissue that surrounds and supports various organs in the body).

The most common forms of sarcoma develop in bone, cartilage, fat, muscle or blood vessels.



**Wilms’ tumour:**

A cancerous (malignant) tumour that starts in the kidney .

Wilms tumours most often develop in young children.

Also called nephroblastoma.
