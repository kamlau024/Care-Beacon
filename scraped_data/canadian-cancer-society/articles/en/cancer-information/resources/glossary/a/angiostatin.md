# angiostatin

## Description

A protein that blocks angiogenesis (the growth of new blood vessels) and is involved in blood clotting.

Angiostatin is found naturally in the body. It can also be produced in a lab.

Angiostatin may be used in anti-angiogenesis therapy.

* * *

**anti-angiogenesis:**

A process that slows or stops the growth of new blood vessels that a tumourneeds to grow and survive.
