# antagonist

## Description

A drug or substance that binds to a receptor and blocks the actions of another substance.

For example, anti-androgen therapy to treat prostate cancer uses antagonists that block the actions of androgens (male sex hormones).

Compare with agonist.

* * *

**agonist:**

A drug or substance that binds to a receptor in or on a cell and causes a specific response or action by the cell.

Compare with antagonist .
