# anticytokine therapy

## Description

Treatment with drugs that stop the production or block the effects of cytokines.

Anticytokine therapy is a type of immunotherapy that reduces inflammation and slows the growth and spread of some tumours.

* * *

**cytokines:**

A substance made by cells of the immune system. Cytokines allow immune system cells to communicate with each other and thus help carry out the body’s immune response (the immune system’s reaction to the presence of foreign substances in the body).

Cytokines are produced by the body or can be made in a lab.



**immunotherapy:**

A treatment that uses and strengthens the immune system to fight disease including cancer.

Immunotherapy is a type of biological therapy .
