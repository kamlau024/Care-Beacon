# antibody therapy

## Description

Treatment with an antibody that attacks cancer cells. Antibody therapy can kill specific tumour cells or stimulate the immune system to kill tumour cells.

* * *

**antibody:**

A type of protein made by the immune system that disarms or destroys a specific foreign substance ( antigen ) when it appears in the body.
