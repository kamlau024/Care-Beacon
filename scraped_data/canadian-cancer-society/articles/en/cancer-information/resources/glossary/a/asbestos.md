# asbestos

## Description

A natural material that is made up of silicate fibres and is used for fireproofing, insulation, building materials and chemical filters.

Exposure to asbestos is known to cause several serious lung diseases, including asbestosis (a chronic lung condition), mesothelioma and lung cancer.

* * *

**mesothelioma:**

A tumour that starts in the mesothelial tissue that lines several body cavities, including the chest ( pleura ) , heart ( pericardium ) and abdominal cavity ( peritoneum ) .

Mesothelioma can be non-cancerous (benign) or cancerous (malignant). Malignant mesothelioma is associated with exposure to asbestos fibres.
