# aromatase inhibitor

## Description

A drug that stops the production or blocks the actions of the enzyme aromatase, which in turn lowers the level of estradiol (a female hormone) in the body.

Aromatase inhibitors are a type of anti-estrogen therapy.

* * *

**enzyme:**

A protein that speeds up certain chemical reactions in the body.

For example, enzymes in the intestines help to digest food.



**anti-estrogen therapy:**

Treatment with drugs that stop the production or block the actions of estrogen ( a female sex hormone).

Anti-estrogen therapy may be used to treat breast cancer.
