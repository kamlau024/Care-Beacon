# aspartate transaminase (AST)

## Description

An enzyme normally present in the liver, heart and muscles.

A high level of AST in the blood may be a sign of liver or heart damage, certain cancers or other diseases.

Also called serum glutamic-oxaloacetic transaminase (SGOT).
