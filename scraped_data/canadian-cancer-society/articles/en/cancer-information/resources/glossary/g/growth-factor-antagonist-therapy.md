# growth factor antagonist therapy

## Description

See growth factor inhibitor therapy.

* * *

**growth factor inhibitor therapy:**

Treatment that tries to block growth factors to prevent cancer cells from growing.

Growth factors occur naturally in the body. Some cancer cells can use growth factors to help their own growth.
