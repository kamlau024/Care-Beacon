# granulocytopenia

## Description

A reduction in the number of granulocytes (a type of white blood cell that helps fight infection).

Granulocytopenia can be caused by certain diseases, including cancer. It may also be a side effect of some cancer treatments, including chemotherapy and radiation therapy.
