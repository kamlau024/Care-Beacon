# gonadotropin

## Description

Any hormone that stimulates the growth and function of the gonads (the testicles in males and the ovaries in females).

Gonadotropins are made by the pituitary gland.

* * *

**hormone:**

A substance that regulates specific body functions, such as metabolism , growth and reproduction.

Natural hormones are produced by glands. Artificial or synthetic hormones can be made in the lab.



**pituitary gland:**

The main endocrine system gland at the base of the brain that produces hormones to control other glands and many body functions, including growth.

Also called the hypophysis.
