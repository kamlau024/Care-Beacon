# gastrin

## Description

A hormone that causes the stomach to release an acid that helps to digest food.

Gastrin is made by cells in the stomach.

* * *

**hormone:**

A substance that regulates specific body functions, such as metabolism , growth and reproduction.

Natural hormones are produced by glands. Artificial or synthetic hormones can be made in the lab.



**stomach:**

The hollow, muscular organ in the abdomen that receives food from the esophagus. It makes digestive juices and mixes these juices with food to break it down into a thin liquid, which then passes it into the duodenum (the first part of the small intestine).

Gastric means referring to or having to do with the stomach, as in gastric polyp.
