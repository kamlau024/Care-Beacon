# genome

## Description

All the genetic or hereditary material of an organism.

The human genome is made up of all the DNA in a person’s 23 pairs of chromosomes.

* * *

**chromosomes:**

The part of a cell that contains DNA ( genetic information).

In humans, each cell contains 23 pairs of chromosomes or 46 chromosomes in total.
