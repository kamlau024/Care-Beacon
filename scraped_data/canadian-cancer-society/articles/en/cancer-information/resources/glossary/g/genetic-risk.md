# genetic risk

## Description

The likelihood that a person has a gene mutation (a permanent change in a gene) that puts them at increased risk for a disease, such as cancer.

* * *

**gene:**

The basic biological unit of heredity passed from parents to a child. Genes are pieces of DNA and determine a particular characteristic of an individual.
