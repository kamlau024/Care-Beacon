# genetic

## Description

Having to do with genes or genetics and usually referring to the effect or structure of genes.

For example, a genetic condition is a disease or disorder caused by a mutation (change) in one or more genes.

* * *

**genes:**

The basic biological unit of heredity passed from parents to a child. Genes are pieces of DNA and determine a particular characteristic of an individual.



**genetics:**

The study of genes and heredity (the passing of genetic information from parents to a child).

Medical genetics involves diagnosing and managing genetic disorders as well as counselling people with these disorders.
