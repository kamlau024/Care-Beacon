# gallium scan

## Description

A procedure that uses gallium (a radioisotope) to produce images of structures and organs inside the body.

Doctors use gallium scan to look for inflammation, an abscess (collection of pus), tumours or other signs of disease. They may also use gallium scans to measure a person’s response to cancer treatment.

Gallium scan is a type of nuclear scan.

* * *

**radioisotope:**

A substance or element that gives off radiation.

Radioisotopes can be used in imaging tests and cancer treatments. When a radioisotope is mixed with a medicine or drug, it is called a radiopharmaceutical.

Also called radioactive isotope.



**inflammation:**

The body’s protective response to injury or infection that includes redness, swelling, pain and warmth of the affected area.



**nuclear scan:**

An imaging technique that uses a small amount of radioactive substance (called a radioisotope ) . The radioisotope is injected into the body and collects in certain tissues. A scanner takes pictures of the radiation given off by the radioisotope and creates an image of the organs or structures.

Also called nuclear medicine scan, radioactive isotope scan or radioisotope scan.
