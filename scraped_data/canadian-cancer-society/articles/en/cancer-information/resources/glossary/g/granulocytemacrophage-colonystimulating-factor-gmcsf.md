# granulocyte-macrophage colony-stimulating factor (GM-CSF)

## Description

A substance that stimulates the bone marrow to develop white blood cells (especially granulocytes and macrophages). These white blood cells help defend the body against bacteria, viruses and types of fungus.

GM-CSF is a cytokine found naturally in the body. It can also be made in a lab.

GM-CSF is a type of biological therapy used in cancer treatment to stimulate the immune system. It may also be given after chemotherapy to help increase white blood cell counts and to lower the risk of infection.

* * *

**cytokine:**

A substance made by cells of the immune system. Cytokines allow immune system cells to communicate with each other and thus help carry out the body’s immune response (the immune system’s reaction to the presence of foreign substances in the body).

Cytokines are produced by the body or can be made in a lab.



**biological therapy:**

Treatment with natural or manufactured substances that change the way cells behave. Biological therapies may cause specific cells to stop growing, block the release of hormones or strengthen the body’s immune system.

Biological therapies are used to fight cancer or lessen the side effects associated with some cancer treatments.

Also called biotherapy or biological response modifiers (BRMs).
