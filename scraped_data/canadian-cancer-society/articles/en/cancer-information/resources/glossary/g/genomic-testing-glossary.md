# genomic testing

## Description

A genomic test uses a sample of blood, tissue or bodily fluid to find genetic changes in cancer cells. This can help predict how quickly cancer will grow or spread, and how well it will respond to treatment.

Genomic testing can also be used to find inherited genetic changes that could increase the risk of developing a disease or condition such as cancer.

* * *

**genetic:**

Having to do with genes or genetics and usually referring to the effect or structure of genes.

For example, a genetic condition is a disease or disorder caused by a mutation (change) in one or more genes.



**inherited:**

Passed from parent to child through information contained in genes .

Also referred to as hereditary.
