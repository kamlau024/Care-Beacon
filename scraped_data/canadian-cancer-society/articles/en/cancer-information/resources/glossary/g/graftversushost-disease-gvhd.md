# graft-versus-host disease (GVHD)

## Description

A condition that might happen after a stem cell transplant. Healthy stem cells from a donor (called the graft) attack a recipient’s (receiver’s) cells (called the host). The graft cells see the host cells as foreign and start to destroy them. Symptoms include jaundice, rash or blisters on the skin, dry mouth or dry eyes.

Compare with graft rejection.

* * *

**stem cell transplant:**

A surgical procedure in which tissue or an organ is transferred from one place in a person’s body to another, or from one person (donor) to another (recipient).



**graft rejection:**

A complication of organ or tissue transplant in which the recipient’s immune system reacts against and attacks the donated tissue (graft).

Compare with graft-versus-host disease (GVHD) .
