# gestational trophoblastic disease

## Description

A rare tumour that starts in the tissues that develop into the placenta during pregnancy and grows in the uterus (womb).

The non-cancerous (benign) form of the disease is called hydatidiform mole( or molar pregnancy). The rarer cancerous (malignant) form is called choriocarcinoma.

Also called gestational trophoblastic neoplasia or gestational trophoblastic tumour.

* * *

**hydatidiform mole:**

A non-cancerous (benign) tumour that starts in the layer of the uterus (womb) that normally develops into the placenta during pregnancy.

Hydatidiform mole is the non-cancerous (benign) form of gestational trophoblastic disease . On rare occasions it can develop into the cancerous (malignant) form ( choriocarcinoma ) .

Also called molar pregnancy.



**choriocarcinoma:**

A cancerous (malignant) tumour that starts in the layer of the uterus (womb) that normally develops into the placenta during pregnancy.

Choriocarcinoma is the cancerous (malignant) form of gestational trophoblastic disease . On rare occasions, choriocarcinoma may develop in a testicle or ovary (these tumours are not the same as gestational choriocarcinoma).

Compare with hydatidiform mole .
