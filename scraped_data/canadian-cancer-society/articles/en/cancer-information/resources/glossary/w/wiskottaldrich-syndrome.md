# Wiskott-Aldrich syndrome

## Description

An

inherited

Close

inherited

Passed from parent to child through information contained in [genes](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/resources/glossary/g/gene).

Also referred to as hereditary.

condition that affects the blood cells and the cells of the immune system. It usually only affects boys. Wiskott-Aldrich syndrome increases the risk of Hodgkin lymphoma, non-Hodgkin lymphoma and acute myelogenous leukemia (AML).
