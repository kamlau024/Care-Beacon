# first-degree relative

## Description

A person’s parents, brothers, sisters or children.

Also called FDR.

Compare with second-degree relative.

* * *

**second-degree relative:**

A person’s aunts, uncles, grandparents, grandchildren, nieces, nephews or half-siblings.

Also called SDR.

Compare with first-degree relative .
