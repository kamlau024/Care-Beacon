# flavonoid

## Description

A substance found citrus fruits (such as grapefruit, oranges, lemons and limes), tea and red wine.

Flavonoids are a type of phytochemical that have antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects. Researchers are studying the role of flavonoids in preventing cancer.

* * *

**antioxidant:**

Any substance that help protect cells from damage caused by free radicals ( unstable molecules made during metabolism, which is the chemical processes needed for cell function, growth and reproduction).



**anti-inflammatory:**

A drug or other substance that prevents or reduces inflammation (redness, swelling, pain and warmth of the affected area).
