# familial atypical multiple mole melanoma (FAMMM) syndrome

## Description

An inherited condition where a person has many moles( usually more than 50) that often look different than normal moles. A person with FAMMM also has one or more close relatives with melanoma.

FAMMM increases the risk of certain cancers, including melanoma skin cancer and pancreatic cancer.

Also called dysplastic nevi syndrome.

* * *

**moles:**

A non-cancerous (benign) growth on the skin that is usually tan, brown or flesh-coloured.

Moles are made up of a cluster of melanocytes (cells that make melanin, which gives skin, hair and eyes their colour). They may be raised or flat.

Also called nevus.



**melanoma:**

A cancerous (malignant) tumour that starts in melanocytes (cells that make melanin, which gives skin, hair and eyes their colour).

Melanoma most often develops in a mole (skin melanoma), but can also develop in the eyes or intestines.
