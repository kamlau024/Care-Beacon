# folate

## Description

A form of vitamin B9 and an essential nutrient that helps the body make new cells and plays a role in the normal development of the nervous system.

Folate is found in liver, green vegetables, oranges, lentils, beans and whole grain breads and cereals.

Also called folic acid.
