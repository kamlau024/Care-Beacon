# family history

## Description

The medical history of a person’s immediate family (first-degree relatives), including mother, father, siblings and grandparents.

A family history shows the pattern of certain diseases in a family and helps determine risk factors for those and other diseases.

* * *

**medical history:**

A record of a person’s present symptoms, risk factors and all the medical events and problems a person has had in the past.
