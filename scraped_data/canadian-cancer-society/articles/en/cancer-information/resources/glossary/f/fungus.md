# fungus

## Description

Any of a variety of plant-like organisms that do not make chlorophyll and reproduce by spores.

Examples of fungi include mushrooms, moulds and yeasts. Fungi can cause infections (for example, a yeast infection) .

Fungal infections are a side effect of some cancer treatments, including chemotherapy and radiation therapy, because these treatments can suppress the immune system.

* * *

**yeast infection:**

A fungal infection that may occur in any of the mucous membranes in the body, including the lining of the mouth, esophagus or vagina.

Yeast infections are a potential side effect of some chemotherapy drugs.

Also called candidiasis or thrush (when it affects the mouth).
