# Fanconi anemia

## Description

An inherited condition that affects the bone marrow so it cannot make red blood cells, white blood cells or platelets.

Fanconi anemia increases the risk of myelodysplastic syndromes (MDS), acute myelogenous leukemia (AML), and oral and oropharyngeal cancers.

* * *

**inherited:**

Passed from parent to child through information contained in genes .

Also referred to as hereditary.



**bone marrow:**

The soft, spongy tissue inside most bones.

There are 2 main types of bone marrow. Red bone marrow is where blast cells (immature blood cells) develop into red blood cells , white blood cells and platelets . Yellow bone marrow stores fatty tissue.
