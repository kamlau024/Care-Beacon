# focal

## Description

Localized, occurring in a specific area or limited to one place.

For example, a focal cancer is limited to one specific organ or area of the body.

Compare with disseminated or multifocal.

* * *

**disseminated:**

Scattered or distributed over a large area.

For example, a disseminated cancer is scattered throughout an organ, tissue or the body.

Compare with focal or multifocal .



**multifocal:**

Occurring in more than one place.

For example, a multifocal cancer starts or appears in more than one area of the body at the same time.

Also called multicentric.

Compare with disseminated or focal .
