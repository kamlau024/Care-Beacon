# Fanconi syndrome

## Description

A disorder of the kidneys that causes them to release certain substances into the urine instead of absorbing them into the bloodstream.

Fanconi syndrome may be caused by a genetic condition or kidney damage.

Compare with Fanconi anemia.

* * *

**Fanconi anemia:**

An inherited condition that affects the bone marrow so it cannot make red blood cells, white blood cells or platelets.

Fanconi anemia increases the risk of myelodysplastic syndromes (MDS), acute myelogenous leukemia (AML), and oral and oropharyngeal cancers.
