# x-linked lymphoproliferative disease (XLP)

## Description

An

inherited

Close

inherited

Passed from parent to child through information contained in [genes](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/resources/glossary/g/gene).

Also referred to as hereditary.

condition that affects the immune system so it has an abnormal response to an Epstein-Barr virus infection. This response can cause damage to the bone marrow, liver, spleen, heart and kidney. It is more common in males. XLP increases the risk of non-Hodgkin lymphoma.
