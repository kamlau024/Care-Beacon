# xeroderma pigmentosum (XP)

## Description

An

inherited

Close

inherited

Passed from parent to child through information contained in [genes](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/resources/glossary/g/gene).

Also referred to as hereditary.

condition that affects the skin so it can’t repair damage caused by ultraviolet radiation from the sun. XP greatly increases the risk of melanoma and non-melanoma skin cancers.
