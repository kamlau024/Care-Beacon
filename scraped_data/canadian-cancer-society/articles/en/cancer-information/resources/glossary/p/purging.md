# purging

## Description

A process in which harvested blood-forming stem cells or bone marrow is treated to kill any unwanted cells.

For example, stem cells can be treated with chemotherapy to kill any cancer cells that may be mixed with them before they are used for stem cell transplant.

* * *

**stem cell transplant:**

Treatment that involves giving stem cells (unspecialized cells), which develop into healthy specialized cells and replace damaged tissue.

For example, stem cell transplant is used to replace blood-forming stems cells in bone marrow damaged or destroyed by disease (such as cancer), drugs or radiation.
