# pelvic floor

## Description

The muscles that make up the lower part of the pelvis. These muscles support the organs inside the pelvis. When pelvic floor muscles don’t work properly, it may cause incontinence.

* * *

**pelvis:**

The lower part of the abdomen between the hip bones that contains the urinary system and reproductive system .

Pelvic means referring to or having to do with the pelvis, as in pelvic exam.



**incontinence:**

Inability to control bowel movements or urination.
