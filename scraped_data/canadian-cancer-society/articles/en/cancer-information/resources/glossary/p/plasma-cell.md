# plasma cell

## Description

A type of white blood cell that produces antibodies to help the body fight infection.

Plasma cells develop from activated B cells (a type of white blood cell that make antibodies to fight bacteria, viruses and fungi). They are part of the body’s immune system.

* * *

**antibodies:**

A type of protein made by the immune system that disarms or destroys a specific foreign substance ( antigen ) when it appears in the body.
