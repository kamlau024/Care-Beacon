# protein

## Description

A molecule that is made up of amino acids and is necessary for the body to grow and repair itself.

Proteins are the basic building blocks for body structures (such as skin and hair) and substances (such as enzymes, cytokines and antibodies) .

* * *

**enzymes:**

A protein that speeds up certain chemical reactions in the body.

For example, enzymes in the intestines help to digest food.



**cytokines:**

A substance made by cells of the immune system. Cytokines allow immune system cells to communicate with each other and thus help carry out the body’s immune response (the immune system’s reaction to the presence of foreign substances in the body).

Cytokines are produced by the body or can be made in a lab.



**antibodies:**

A type of protein made by the immune system that disarms or destroys a specific foreign substance ( antigen ) when it appears in the body.
