# prostate-specific antigen (PSA) failure

## Description

See biochemical recurrence.

* * *

**biochemical recurrence:**

The prostate-specific antigen (PSA) level starts to rise quickly after treatment for prostate cancer but there are no other signs of cancer.

Also called PSA failure.
