# pulmonary fibrosis

## Description

A condition in which lung tissue is replaced with fibrous (scar) tissue. Symptoms include shortness of breath, coughing and a lower tolerance for physical activity.

Pulmonary fibrosis can be a side effect of some cancer treatments, including chemotherapy and radiation therapy.
