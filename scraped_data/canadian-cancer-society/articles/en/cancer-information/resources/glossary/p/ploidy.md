# ploidy

## Description

The number of sets of chromosomes in a cell.

See also haploid, diploid and trisomy.

* * *

**chromosomes:**

The part of a cell that contains DNA ( genetic information).

In humans, each cell contains 23 pairs of chromosomes or 46 chromosomes in total.



**haploid:**

Having one half of a chromosome pair. In humans, sperm and egg cells are haploid and have 23 chromosomes.

Compare with diploid .



**diploid:**

Having a complete (both halves of) a chromosome pair. In humans, most cells are diploid and have 46 chromosomes (23 from the mother and 23 from the father).

Compare with haploid .



**trisomy:**

Having 3 copies of a chromosome rather than the normal chromosome pair.

For example, Down syndrome is caused by the presence of an extra chromosome 21 (trisomy 21).
