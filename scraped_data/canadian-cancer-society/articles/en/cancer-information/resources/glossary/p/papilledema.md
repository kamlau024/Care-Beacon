# papilledema

## Description

Swelling of the optic disc (the circular area on the retina where nerve fibres come together to form the optic nerve) .

Papilledema is caused by increased pressure within the brain due to a tumour or other problem.

* * *

**optic nerve:**

The nerve that carries messages from the retina (nerve tissue at the back of the eye that receives images) to the brain.
