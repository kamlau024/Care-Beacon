# Peutz-Jeghers syndrome

## Description

An inherited condition that causes dark spots on the mouth and fingers and polyps in the large and small intestine.

Peutz-Jeghers syndrome increases the risk of colorectal, small intestine, stomach, pancreatic, breast and other cancers.

* * *

**inherited:**

Passed from parent to child through information contained in genes .

Also referred to as hereditary.
