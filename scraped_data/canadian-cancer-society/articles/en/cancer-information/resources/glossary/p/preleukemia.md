# preleukemia

## Description

See myelodysplastic syndrome (MDS).

* * *

**myelodysplastic syndrome (MDS):**

A group of diseases where the bone marrow doesn't make enough healthy mature blood cells. The different types of MDS are considered cancer and they are sometimes called bone marrow failure diseases. Sometimes an MDS will develop into acute myeloid leukemia .
