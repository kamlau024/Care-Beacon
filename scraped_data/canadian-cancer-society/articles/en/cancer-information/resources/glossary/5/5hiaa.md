# 5-HIAA

## Description

See 5-hydroxyindoleactic acid (5-HIAA).

* * *

**5-hydroxyindoleactic acid (5-HIAA):**

A substance produced when serotonin breaks down.

5-HIAA can be used as a tumour marker . It is used to look for tumours that start in cells of the neuroendocrine system or to monitor a person’s response to treatment for neuroendocrine tumours.
