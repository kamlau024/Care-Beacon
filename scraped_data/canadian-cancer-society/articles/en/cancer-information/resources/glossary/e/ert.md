# ERT

## Description

See estrogen replacement therapy (ERT).

* * *

**estrogen replacement therapy (ERT):**

Treatment that replaces estrogen ( a female sex hormone) when it is no longer produced by the ovaries.

ERT may be given to women who are postmenopausal or have had their ovaries surgically removed.

Also called estrogen therapy.
