# exclusion criteria

## Description

Conditions or factors that are used to decide who can't participate in, or who must be excluded from, a clinical trial.

Exclusion criteria are often listed with a clinical trial’s eligibility criteria.

Compare with inclusion criteria.

* * *

**eligibility criteria:**

Conditions or factors that determine if a person is eligible for, or meets the requirements to participate in, a clinical trial .

Eligibility criteria ensure that participants in a clinical trial share similar traits, such as age, sex, type and stage of cancer, general health and previous treatment. This helps ensure that the study’s results will be scientifically valid.

See also inclusion criteria and exclusion criteria



**inclusion criteria:**

Conditions or factors that make a person eligible for and suited to participate in a clinical trial.

Inclusion criteria are often listed with a clinical trial’s eligibility criteria .

Compare with exclusion criteria .
