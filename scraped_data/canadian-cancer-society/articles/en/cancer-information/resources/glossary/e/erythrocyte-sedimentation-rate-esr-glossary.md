# erythrocyte sedimentation rate (ESR)

## Description

A lab test that measures how quickly red blood cells fall to the bottom of a test tube. Red blood cells fall faster than normal when there is inflammation in the body.

Inflammation can be caused by some cancers, immune system problems or blood disorders.

Also called a sed rate.

* * *

**red blood cells:**

A type of blood cell that carries oxygen to and carbon dioxide from tissues in the body. Red blood cells contain hemoglobin (a protein that carries oxygen and gives blood its red colour) and are made in the bone marrow.

Also called erythrocyte.



**inflammation:**

The body’s protective response to injury or infection that includes redness, swelling, pain and warmth of the affected area.



**immune system:**

The complex group of cells and organs that defend the body against infection, disease and foreign substances.
