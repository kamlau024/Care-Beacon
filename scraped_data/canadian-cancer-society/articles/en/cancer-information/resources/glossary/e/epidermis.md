# epidermis

## Description

The upper or outer layer of skin (above the dermis) that is made up of squamous cells and basal cells. It also contains melanocytes (cells that make melanin, which gives skin its colour).

_Epidermal_ means referring to or having to do with the epidermis, as in epidermal cell. _Epidermoid_ means made up of or resembling epidermal tissue, as in epidermoid carcinoma.

* * *

**squamous cells:**

A type of epithelial cell that is thin and flat and looks like a fish scale.

Squamous cells are found in the epithelium that makes up the surface of the skin. They are in the epithelium lining of organs such as the mouth, pharynx, esophagus, anus, cervix and vagina. Squamous cells also make up the lining of blood vessels and hollow areas of the body (called cavities).



**basal cells:**

A small, round cell found in the deepest part of the epidermis (the outer layer of the skin).
