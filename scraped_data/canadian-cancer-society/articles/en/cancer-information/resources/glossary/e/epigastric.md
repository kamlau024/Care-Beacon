# epigastric

## Description

Epigastric refers to or having to do with the upper middle area of the abdomen. It also refers to or having to do with structures lying on or closely related to the stomach, including the lower part of the esophagus.

* * *

**stomach:**

The hollow, muscular organ in the abdomen that receives food from the esophagus. It makes digestive juices and mixes these juices with food to break it down into a thin liquid, which then passes it into the duodenum (the first part of the small intestine).

Gastric means referring to or having to do with the stomach, as in gastric polyp.



**esophagus:**

The muscular tube in the neck and chest through which food passes from the pharynx (throat) to the stomach.

Esophageal means referring to or having to do with the esophagus, as in esophageal cancer.

Also called the gullet.
