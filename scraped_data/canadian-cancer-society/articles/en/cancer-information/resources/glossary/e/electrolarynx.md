# electrolarynx

## Description

See artificial larynx.

* * *

**artificial larynx:**

A battery-operated machine held against the throat to create vibration to help people speak after their larynx (voice box) is removed.

An electrolarynx is an example of an artificial larynx.

Also called prosthetic voice box or mechanical larynx.
