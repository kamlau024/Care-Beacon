# enzyme inhibitor therapy

## Description

Treatment with drugs that stop the production or block the actions of certain enzymes in the body.

* * *

**enzymes:**

A protein that speeds up certain chemical reactions in the body.

For example, enzymes in the intestines help to digest food.
