# endostatin

## Description

A substance that blocks angiogenesis (the growth of new blood vessels).

Endostatin is a type of cytokine that is made by cells in the immune system. It can also be produced in the lab.

Endostatin may be used in anti-angiogenesis therapy.

* * *

**cytokine:**

A substance made by cells of the immune system. Cytokines allow immune system cells to communicate with each other and thus help carry out the body’s immune response (the immune system’s reaction to the presence of foreign substances in the body).

Cytokines are produced by the body or can be made in a lab.



**anti-angiogenesis:**

A process that slows or stops the growth of new blood vessels that a tumourneeds to grow and survive.
