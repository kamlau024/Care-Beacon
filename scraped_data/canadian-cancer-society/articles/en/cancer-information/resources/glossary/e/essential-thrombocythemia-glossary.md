# essential thrombocythemia

## Description

A blood disease where the bone marrow makes too many platelets. ​It is one of a group of blood cancers known as myeloproliferative neoplasm.

Too many platelets in the blood can form a blood clot (thrombus) and can cause a stroke, a heart attack or blood clots in the lungs (pulmonary embolism). In rare cases, it can also cause bleeding problems.

* * *

**bone marrow:**

The soft, spongy tissue inside most bones.

There are 2 main types of bone marrow. Red bone marrow is where blast cells (immature blood cells) develop into red blood cells , white blood cells and platelets . Yellow bone marrow stores fatty tissue.



**platelets:**

A type of blood cell that helps blood to clot. Platelets are made in the bone marrow.

Also called thrombocyte.
