# exocrine gland

## Description

A type of gland that releases substances to the surface of the body or into other organs through a duct.

Examples of exocrine glands include the pancreas, salivary glands, sweat glands and mammary glands.

* * *

**gland:**

A specialized organ or group of cells that produces or releases substances (such as hormones , saliva, digestive juices, sweat, tears or milk) to perform different functions in the body.
