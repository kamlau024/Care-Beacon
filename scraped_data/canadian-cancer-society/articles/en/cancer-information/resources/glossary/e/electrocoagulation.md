# electrocoagulation

## Description

A procedure that uses a strong electric current to heat tissue so it coagulates, or clumps together, to form a shapeless mass.

Electrocoagulation is used to destroy abnormal tissue or cancer cells. It is also used to seal off blood vessels to stop bleeding.
