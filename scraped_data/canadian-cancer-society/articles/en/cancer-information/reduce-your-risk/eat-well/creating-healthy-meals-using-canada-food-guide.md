# Creating healthy meals using Canada’s Food Guide

## Eat a variety of healthy foods each day

[Canada’s Food Guide](https://food-guide.canada.ca/en/) is a handy tool that helps you make food choices and develop healthy eating habits. The food guide recommends that you eat a variety of healthy foods each day. This includes plenty of vegetables and fruit, whole grain foods and protein foods:

  * Make half your plate vegetables and fruit. Vegetables and fruit should make up the largest proportion of the foods you eat throughout the day.
  * Make a quarter of your plate whole grain foods.
  * Make a quarter of your plate protein foods. Choose protein foods that come from plants more often.

Check out [Canada’s Food Guide resources](https://food-guide.canada.ca/en/healthy-eating-resources/) to learn more about food choices and eating habits. Don’t forget that the food guide is just that – a guide. The foods on your plate at every meal don’t need to be as varied as Canada’s Food Guide plate. But do try for variety, balance and flexibility over time.
