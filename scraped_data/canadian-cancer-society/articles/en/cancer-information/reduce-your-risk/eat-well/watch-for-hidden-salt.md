# Watch for hidden salt

## Too much salt may increase your risk of which type of cancer?

Eating too much salt and salty food may increase your risk of stomach cancer.

![Body showing that eating too much salt and salty food can raise the risk of stomach cancer](https://cdn.cancer.ca/-/media/images/cancer-information/reduce-your-risk/eat-well/prev-2021_salt-en.png?rev=7bf6be4957b640e7b3dd8077061a3d50&cx=0.5&cy=0.5&cw=575&ch=452&hash=5F43DEDFC9361354291A398606CB02CC)

## How much salt should we use?

According to Health Canada, most Canadians need 1500 mg of salt per day. Our bodies need some salt to help maintain our balance of water (hydration). Also, table salt is fortified with iodine, which you need for good health.

But 3 out of 5 Canadians eat almost double the amount of salt needed in a day.

A lot of the salt we eat comes from processed food such as breakfast cereals, soups, sauces, bread, ready-made meals and snack foods (where it can be listed as sodium). We also add salt to our food at the table or when cooking.
