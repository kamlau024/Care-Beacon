# Be safe at work

Cancer-causing substances at work are responsible for a small percentage of cancers. Know your risk and protect yourself.

## Other

Along with solar radiation and asbestos, diesel engine exhaust and crystalline silica have the largest estimated impact on cancer burden related to workplace risk exposure.
