# Live smoke-free

Living smoke-free is the best thing you can do to reduce your risk for cancer.

Did you know that smoking is responsible for an estimated 30% of all cancer deaths in Canada? About 72% of lung cancer cases are due to smoking tobacco.

## Cigarettes: the hard truth

The more you smoke and the longer you smoke, the more you increase your risk of developing lung and other cancers. In fact, any smoking at all may cause lung damage.

[ Find out more ](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/reduce-your-risk/live-smoke-free/cigarettes-the-hard-truth)

## Quitting is possible

It’s never too late to quit! No matter how old you are or how long you’ve been smoking, you can reduce your risk of cancer by quitting.

[ Find out more ](https://cancer.ca/en/cancer-information/reduce-your-risk/live-smoke-free/quitting-is-possible)
