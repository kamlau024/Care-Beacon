# Cancer Statistics Online Dashboard

Page Content

The following statistics are available on the [BC Cancer Statistics Online Dashboard](https://bccandataanalytics.shinyapps.io/BCSummary/ "BC Cancer Statistics Online Dashboard ") for you to access, customize and download:

  * Cancer incidence
  * Cancer mortality

  * Cancer prevalence
  * Projected number of new cancer diagnoses
  * Outcomes
  * Create your own customizable table
