# Practical Support

Counselling and resources for self-management are available for patients and their families.

## Counselling

Your health care team wants you to have all the information and support you need to deal with your cancer diagnosis and treatment. You might find you need a counsellor to assess your situation and provide referrals to programs or services.

You can make a counselling appointment with [Patient & Family Counselling Services](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/_layouts/15/FIXUPREDIRECT.ASPX?WebId=21dc1809-d513-425f-bc0b-66947486704a&TermSetId=4b055df4-ab24-43a2-a7f2-2dfd42c28147&TermId=3e7fac77-7f80-4c9a-8157-2708ae5752f5 "Learn more about Patient & Family Counselling Services at BC Cancer") at your local cancer centre.

The [Practical & Financial Needs Self-assessment](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/coping-and-support-site/Documents/Support%20Programs/Practical_%20FinancialNeeds_SelfAssessment.pdf "Download the Practical & Financial Needs Self-assessment ") checklist may help you decide what practical and financial resources might be available for you.
