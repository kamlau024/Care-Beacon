# Work & School

Cancer and its treatments can affect your work or education plans. You may need some support and information. A vocational rehabilitation counsellor can help.

Page Content

For resources, workshops and more information about our vocational rehabilitation counsellor, please visit our [Vocational Rehabilitation page](https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/_layouts/15/FIXUPREDIRECT.ASPX?WebId=21dc1809-d513-425f-bc0b-66947486704a&TermSetId=4b055df4-ab24-43a2-a7f2-2dfd42c28147&TermId=07f93252-6667-4d91-837c-970a90748992 "opens bc cancer vocational rehabilitation page in new tab").
